## In brief

- Provenance and evidence status are different axes; tool status is a third concept.
- “Observed” never means “real” when a fact is Synthetic or Simulated.
- “Not measured” is a coverage label, never zero and not an evidence status.
- A release contains statistics and Public records, plus the register-company list, which gives each register company's employee count from its attached records ([decision 0022](decisions/0022-real-register-companies-with-a-synthetic-world.md); ARC-131); every other record-level synthetic input stays in the build vault. Named synthetic firms are retired.
- The application calculates and renders. Models select catalogue entries; they do not write prose, queries or numbers.
- Only figures from the four protected record types snap to release units; the Public and Simulated layers of a drawn area are computed for its exact shape ([decision 0016](decisions/0016-version-1-covers-the-whole-initiative.md)), except a movement split (see **Movement splits**).
- No sex or gender attribute is added on the worker, residence or company records or on anything derived from them. Movement aggregates are the one exception ([decision 0023](decisions/0023-ten-additions-and-the-data-rule.md) item 16): they carry age band, gender and nationality group as one-way splits. Nationality enters in three forms only: the nationality group of the synthetic worker records, the register's establishment nationality ([decision 0022](decisions/0022-real-register-companies-with-a-synthetic-world.md)), and the same groups plus international visitors on movement aggregates, each only as a dimension of released statistics, except that the establishment nationality the public register publishes for a company is also a Public field of its company card ([decision 0022](decisions/0022-real-register-companies-with-a-synthetic-world.md) item 4). An official split by sex or nationality is a Public fact only ([decision 0017](decisions/0017-public-data-additions-and-public-splits.md)); the movement generator may use such figures, summed over the source's own categories, as cited anchors of its splits. Whole cells of a published population grid by age and sex may be summed over an area, as a Public fact and for display only ([decision 0019](decisions/0019-forecasts-estimates-scenarios-and-self-serve-sources.md) item 22).
- A forecast, an estimate or a base rate is the output of tested code with its interval, range or counts, method and source; a user's scenario is the user's own and is never called a forecast; nothing forecasts the success of a venture ([decision 0019](decisions/0019-forecasts-estimates-scenarios-and-self-serve-sources.md)).
- Records are generated only for the confirmed sources of the data rule: the employment of private-sector individuals, wages and residence attached to the register companies (Synthetic), and movement, with the three splits of item 16 (Simulated); every other input is public data or a company's own input, and no synthetic data stands in for any other source ([decision 0023](decisions/0023-ten-additions-and-the-data-rule.md) item 12).
- The product is for companies only, designed for three personas (see **Persona**). Version 1 has no oversight seat and no ministry-only view; the two company seats stay apart, and the ledger is kept only as the internal **Question log**, with no screen ([decision 0023](decisions/0023-ten-additions-and-the-data-rule.md) item 15).
- Terms are requirements for the planned product, not claims that it exists.

## A

- **Accounting gate**: The deterministic audit before display, labelled on the receipt as "Checked before display". It runs five checks—Sources, Assumptions, Nothing invented, Scope, Limits stated—on the complete structured brief; failure selects the template writer and is logged.
- **Activity spine**: The reviewed mapping among commerce activity code, ISIC Rev.4 class and public place category, with unmapped and not-mappable cases visible. The category tree of the Foursquare places dataset gets its own reviewed mapping ([decision 0019](decisions/0019-forecasts-estimates-scenarios-and-self-serve-sources.md) item 21).
- **Additive family**: One release table, one partition, one measure where the table has measures, and one breakdown. Inside it the leaves are mutually exclusive and treated once, and every stored total is the exact sum of that family's own leaves. Equality is not required between families (PRV-29, PRV-71).
- **Address search**: The live search for an address in the ask bar and the place picker, offered only while `ESRI_API_KEY` is present and the presenter's offline control is not set. The browser sends the typed text only to the local server, which alone asks the imagery provider and passes the result on; the map flies to the result, which is never written to a file, the database, the ledger, an egress record or a log and never reaches the model. A pin the user then places is the user's own pin. Without the key, or offline, places resolve through the gazetteer only (decision 0023 item 7; AI-130, FE-142).
- **Apportioning**: Post-processing in the product plane that spreads a released value over a drawn shape by a fixed weight: a released Simulated presence count at release-unit grain, or a Public statistic published on an official geography. A Public statistic apportioned to a shape is labelled Inferred and names its method and its geography. Figures from the protected record types are never apportioned (they snap), and no tabulated Public split by sex or nationality is apportioned to a drawn area. The sum of whole cells of a published population grid over an area is not apportioning (see **Whole-cell sum**). The presence count this term spreads is an eligible unsplit presence measure. A movement split is never apportioned to a drawn outline: its values are sums over the snapped whole units, with their `other` values, and a share is over the split's own summed total. The weight is fixed in document 07 (decision 0016).
- **Area**: The geography of an answer, built from a pin, a drawn shape, a radius or a walk or drive time. Figures from the four protected record types (business register, employment, residence, wages) are read only after the area is snapped to whole release units; Public and Simulated layers are computed for the exact shape, except a movement split (see **Movement splits**) (decision 0016).
- **Artefact**: A non-table file listed and hashed in a release manifest, such as a routing graph or signed build report.
- **ASSUMPTION**: A labelled default chosen where no binding decision exists. It is not evidence and needs the named decision or test stated with it.
- **Atom**: A leaf atom or one of its derived totals inside one additive family. An `other` row is a treated leaf where pooling occurred, not a derived total. The atom's population is the set of contributors it counts.
- **Attached records**: The synthetic employment, wage and residence records attached to a register company by its registration number, in the tables, fields and formats of the real custodian records, the nationality group of each worker included. Every person in them is invented, and their totals follow the calibration table. They are treated everywhere as real. A company's card shows only its employee count from them; its wages and other labour and residence details appear only in aggregates released under document 06 and in the company's own seat (decision 0022; decision 0023 item 15, which removes the oversight seat).
- **Attack suite**: Typed recovery predicates (PRV-116) that must succeed with controls off and fail with controls on. Both parameter sets are evaluated inside the custodian plane; exactly one protected table set is exported.
- **Attacker-view audit**: The one release-gating acceptance model (PRV-112 to PRV-116, PRV-121). Every published leaf is fixed to its untreated aggregate. Signed knowledge fixtures are checked against sealed truth before use, and a published leaf is constrained as having passed its rules. Targets the model cannot tell apart form one target class and are audited once. The attacker holds every row of every Public release table (PRV-03), among them the registration and cancellation dates of register companies, so the model gives a company no jobs before its registration month or after its cancellation month (PRV-113 item 13; decision 0023 item 12). A time budget is set from a measured run at the start of slice S5. What the layout tells about a population that failed a rule is not in the model. The audit is evidence under a stated attacker, never a proof. Its configuration is `artefacts/attacker_view_audit.json`; public totals are `artefacts/attacker_view_public_totals.json`.

## B

- **Backtest**: The rolling-origin run of method card C30 over the past values of one published series: each candidate model of the closed, versioned list is fitted to earlier values and scored on the values that followed, and the candidate with the smallest error by one fixed measure is kept. A series too short for the backtest minimum gets `NO_DATA` with the reason `series_too_short`, never a model without a backtest (decision 0019 item 3).
- **Base cell**: A cell of the fixed H3 frame at resolution 9 in Greater Doha (see **Greater Doha**) and resolution 8 elsewhere.
- **Base rate**: A share of past Public licences of one activity on the source's own geography, never for a drawn area: entry or closure, from method card C31, a flow over the published active stock of the same activity and geography, labelled approximate and shown with its two counts and its period. The survival base rate returns `NO_DATA` with the reason `no_denominator`, because the Public licence tables do not link a cancellation to its issue, until a Public source does. A base rate is a Public fact with evidence status Observed, the status of the churn rate of card C2 (fixed arithmetic over published counts, caveat `calculated`), and it carries the caveat key `base_rate_not_your_chance`. It is worded as a share of past licences and never as the chance that the user's own venture survives, and is never computed from `month_change` or from the openings and closures of register companies (decision 0019 item 7; DAT-159, decision 0023 item 12).
- **Best-location ranking**: Candidate areas for an activity ranked by tested code in `eii_core.calc`, with every criterion, its weight and its value visible, weights the user can change, a rank-stability sentence beside every rank and a public-only mode (method card C11). It is never a verdict, a probability, a confidence claim, a forecast or advice to act (AGENTS.md rule 25). A forecast, a base rate or the site estimate may be one of its criteria, visible with its value and weight and removable by the user (decision 0019 item 13).
- **Best three sites**: The three sites that a location-allocation model, chosen by the user from a closed list (maximal coverage or p-median with three sites, as PySAL `spopt` solves them), picks from candidates of one kind over criteria and ranges the user can see and change, with their shared reach with the company's own branches, never called lost sales (method card C40). Set cover never chooses the sites: beside them it reports only, as a count, the fewest eligible candidates that cover all demand within the catchment limit, or `NO_DATA` with the reason `not_coverable` (MTH-114). Where the user gives no rent, the guide and recorded prices of the candidate's district stand in as a cost range labelled as prices, never as rents. It is never a verdict, a probability or advice to act, and it is shown with its **How sure** sentence (decision 0023 item 5; MTH-114).
- **Brief**: The answer column and its structured selection of claims, caveats, next questions, layout and stage directives.
- **Build machine**: The computer that runs public fetching, synthetic generation, privacy treatment, release building and verification.

## C

- **Calculation layer**: Deterministic tested code in `eii_core.calc`, the release build, or a fixed application-schema read function listed by the data contracts and covered by tests. Every displayed number and comparison comes from one of those three places; nothing else computes, rounds or compares a number, and no model ever does.
- **Calculated import unit value**: The deterministic Public customs ratio of import value to net mass, expressed in QAR per kg. The valuation basis appears as the source states it or as “not stated by the source”; display baskets are defined at eight digits or finer. It is not a landed cost.
- **Calibration manifest**: The generator's complete parameter register. Each parameter is ANCHORED to cited public evidence or ASSUMED with a range and sensitivity sweep; the totals of the attached records follow the calibration table instead (decision 0022). It holds the pooled hourly-shape anchor and the constants of the weather modulation (decision 0019 items 18 and 23).
- **Calibration table**: The table of the owner's note that gives the totals of the attached records by activity, size band, occupation group and salary level; the world build follows it exactly. It is kept outside the repository and read only by the world build in the lead's own shell, and it is the one real custodian-type input (decision 0022).
- **Canary**: A planted synthetic marker or truth used to prove that attacks and leak scans have teeth. It must be recoverable with controls off and unrecoverable with controls on. A company-level canary, such as the entrant canary of the known-entrant attack on a company's pay (a register company whose registration month falls in the window), is chosen from what the register shows and labelled in the scenario, never planted on a real company; in the invented stand-in register of the tests, an invented company that opens in one month of the window plays that part (PRV-75; decision 0023 item 12).
- **Case**: One of four illustrative company situations, each a guided story with a one-click start: case C1, an industrial services firm testing local demand; case C2, a technology supplier choosing whom to approach first; case C3, a distributor weighing a second depot; case C4, a retail chain with a weak branch. Cases C3 and C4 load a sample file marked Synthetic that stands for the company's own records and stays inside the session and seat that loaded it. Not to be confused with the method cards C1 to C41 of document 07 (decision 0016; decision 0019; decision 0023).
- **Catalogue version**: The combined hash of the claim, component, fragment and question catalogues, stored in every brief and included in its memo key.
- **Catchment-share scenario**: The user's scenario of method card C34: customers choose between the user's outlet and the real competitors from public listings, by distance and by an attractiveness that the user sets for the user's outlet against the competitors as a group, or by competitor category, never for one named business. It shows the share of the user's outlet and the share of the competitors, split by competitor category and by distance band (a cell that holds a single competitor is merged into the next wider cell). Where a single competitor is still left after those merges, no split is shown, only the share of the user's outlet and the share of the competitors together; with one competitor in all, only the share of the user's outlet is shown and no share is stated for the competitors. It never names a business per cell; every assumption is visible and editable; it is never called a forecast (decision 0019 item 9).
- **Claim catalogue**: The versioned application data that defines permitted Observed and Inferred claims, subjects, fact slots, caveats, strengths and bilingual templates.
- **Clock**: The time control of all of Qatar in 3D (decision 0023 item 2; FE-143). A time scrubber moves the clock, which drives sunlight with shadows (experimental in deck.gl and drawn only inside one focus district), the map's light and sky, and night lights at dusk; it changes no figure, and the hourly layers still step through stored hours only. Buildings stand at their own heights in the order of PUB-106, the recorded height first, labelled as estimates. It is not the optional **Three-dimensional (3D) view**, which extrudes release units.
- **Company card**: The card of one register company: every field the register publishes for it, person names excluded, and its employee count from its attached records. The map's establishment points and the company lists open it. It never shows the company's wages or another labour or residence detail, and showing it is not a lookup (decision 0022).
- **Component catalogue**: The closed, versioned set of evidence cards and interface components with typed schemas. Components accept fact ids, release unit ids and enumerations, never free numbers, code, HTML, styles or URLs.
- **Conformance suite**: Contract tests that every synthetic or future real adapter must pass unchanged before a feed or release is accepted.
- **Contributor**: The person, job or establishment population behind an atom, defined on a residence, workplace or register basis.
- **Covered earner**: A person in an eligible covered private-sector job. People outside that coverage are not measured, not zero.
- **Coverage**: A declaration of what a feed or figure includes and excludes. Missing coverage is shown explicitly and never converted to zero.
- **Crowd density**: Estimated people present per square kilometre, drawn on the map by hour of day. It is Simulated and computed by tested code from released counts (decision 0016).
- **Custodian**: The body that holds a record type under its own mandate. The pack uses neutral record-type names and does not imply supply or endorsement.
- **Custodian plane**: The offline build environment and the only place record-level synthetic data may exist. Only a release leaves it.

## D

- **Data rule**: The owner's rule of decision 0023 item 12: only data the project is sure to have or get is used. Synthetic records are built, in the real formats and treated as real, only for the confirmed ministry sources: the employment records of private-sector individuals with their occupations and nationality group, the wages and the residence addresses, all attached to the register companies (see **Attached records**), and movement at the grain the pack plans (by area and hour, with origin and destination, carrying the three splits of item 16; Simulated). Every other input is public data, held or fetched, or the company's own input (the files of cases C3 and C4). No synthetic data stands in for rents or tenancies, vacancy, card spending, permit or approval status, school capacity or surveys. No attribute is generated that the confirmed sources do not plausibly hold (employment outside the private sector, worksites, trip purpose), and no legal entity or employer group linking real companies, address type, or opening or closing month: the register's own structure, address type and dates are used where a register file shows them (see **Employer group**, **What changed**). A public anchor published by sex enters every generator other than the movement generator only as a total over the source's categories; the movement generator's age and gender shares, and the share of international visitors, are the exception, anchored on cited Public figures summed over the source's own categories (`mob.split_anchor.population_age_sex` and `mob.split_anchor.visitor_arrivals`; item 16), and the other nationality groups follow the simulated people and are anchored on no Public split.
- **Decision class**: One of five closed offline gateway jobs: `spine_match`, `router_miss_finder`, `brief_second_reader`, `question_route` or `string_parity`.
- **Decision journey**: One of the seven company decisions of decision 0016, journey D1 (whether to enter Qatar) to journey D7 (how to serve the region), each ending with its own answer.
- **Decision question bank**: The bank of 109 decision questions for 16 kinds of user of decision 0023 item 9, less its ministries' group, which item 15 removes. Its 15 remaining groups, from companies coming to Qatar to advertising, seed the question catalogue and the golden and exceed evaluation sets, and each question entered names the personas it serves (AI-129, AI-132). Every question gets a playbook or is recorded as a gap with its reason; the questions that need the joined labour, residence and movement records lead the final comparison, and the bank's own gaps wait for the owner's word (decision 0023 items 9 and 15).
- **Decision record**: A numbered amendment record explaining context, decision, evidence and consequences. In the gateway context, it instead means one JSON audit record under `evals/typed_decisions/runs/`; the two are not the same artefact.
- **Dial**: The control that selects a stored situation and summer setting for estimated people present. It is not a clock and has no intermediate positions.
- **Dial situation**: One of `weekday_lunch`, `weekday_evening`, `friday_evening` or `ramadan_iftar`; summer applies only to the first three.
- **District**: An official public geography below municipality. Public district figures are never blended into protected grid statistics.
- **Dominance**: A disclosure risk when one employer group, the largest two groups, or one person accounts for too much of a unit's earners, establishments or wage bill.
- **Door**: A user's pin at a place, from which an area is made by a radius, a walk time or a drive time. For figures from the four protected record types the pin is first replaced by the centre of its base cell and the area snaps to whole release units; Public and Simulated layers use the exact shape, except a movement split (see **Movement splits**) (PRV-17, decision 0016).
- **Drawn area (geofence)**: An area the user draws, sets by a radius, or picks as a walk or drive time from any point, by hour of day. Public point layers and Simulated layers are computed for its exact shape, except a movement split (see **Movement splits**); only figures from the four protected record types snap to whole release units, with the snap sentence (DES-101), before they are read (decision 0016; decision 0006 as amended). No drive-time catchment goes beyond 30 minutes (see **Thirty-minute cap**).
- **DuckDB**: The release build engine and the embedded read-only standby. Parquet remains the source of truth and the embedded file is disposable.

## E

- **Egress record**: The append-only record of an attempted outbound model request, written before response content is used: the exact payload bytes and their hash for a request that is sent, and only the hash of the proposed payload, with no payload bytes, for a request that the egress scan blocks (AI-77, AI-82).
- **Empirical interval**: The range around a forecast, built from the backtest's own errors at each horizon. Its caption gives two counts as Fact chips: how many of the past values of the backtest fell inside the range that the other past errors gave, out of how many; each past value is checked against a range built without its own error. It is never called a probability or a confidence level and is never said to hold for the next value (decision 0019 item 4).
- **Employer group**: Establishments rolled up through legal entity to the group used for dominance tests. For the register companies a legal entity is the main registration with the branches the register shows under it, otherwise one registration alone, and each legal entity is its own employer group: no group linking real companies is invented. Hidden groups of several legal entities with no shared identifier, and legal entities with several establishments, are planted only in the invented stand-in register of the tests, where every planted dominance case stays (decision 0023 item 12; SYN-22, SYN-23). Its identifier never leaves the vault.
- **Estimate on simulated inputs**: The label of the site demand and revenue estimate of method card C33 while any of its inputs is Simulated or Synthetic. For an area or pin that the user chose it gives a low, a middle and a high revenue, fixed percentiles of the model's own seeded draws and worded as cases of the model, with its main drivers. Its subject is the `area`; it is never an estimate for a named real building or business (decision 0019 item 8).
- **Estimated people present**: Simulated people present inside an area, hour by hour, for each dial situation, the summer switch and the three event days, shown as an estimated count with a range and split into workers, residents and visitors. It is released only at release-unit grain under the panel minimum, complementary suppression and rounding of document 06, and, as an eligible unsplit presence measure, apportioned to a drawn shape in the product plane. A movement split is never apportioned to a drawn outline (see **Movement splits**). The wording is "estimated people around this location", never "footfall of" a named building or business. It replaces the presence index (decision 0016). For a whole release unit it can also be shown by pay band (see **People present by pay band**).
- **Event day**: One of three simulated day types, National Day, a day in Ramadan and a major match at a stadium, each with its own hour-by-hour profile of estimated people present; Simulated (decision 0016).
- **Evidence card**: A governed display of one evidence block with Fact chips, class marks, coverage and required caveats.
- **Evidence drawer**: The one-tap view of a fact's provenance, evidence status, inputs, rules, coverage and source chain.
- **Evidence status**: How an answer uses a fact: exactly Observed, Inferred or Still to test. “Not measured” is a separate coverage label, not an evidence status.
- **Experience plane**: The browser application in `web/`. It renders released facts and cannot calculate statistics or reach the vault.
- **Export register**: The committed append-only file `infra/export_register.jsonl`. The first `./eii freeze` run appends one line with these seven fields in order: `window_key`, `protected_sha256`, `world_seed`, `rules_version_id`, `parameter_set`, `release_id`, `code_version`. Continuous integration checks this file alone: existing lines unchanged, one `protected_sha256` per `window_key`, and one `window_key` per `world_seed`. The second no-flag freeze run builds and verifies the application archive from a base-branch commit containing the line and marks `freeze.json` complete. A frozen bundle can be installed or started only when its descriptor is complete and the register inside the installed application holds its line. No signed receipt file is added (PRV-120).

## F

- **Fact**: The governed object that carries a value, unit, period, geography, provenance, input classes, evidence status, reliability, coverage, caveats, labels, lineage and release.
- **Fact chip**: The only interface component family allowed to render a numeral. It shows the fact's value and marks and opens its Evidence drawer.
- **Fact id**: `f_` plus 32 hexadecimal characters derived from the canonical release id, metric key, geography, period and qualifiers.
- **FDI project**: One announced investment project from the national investment promotion agency's FDI projects dashboard (data from fDi Markets), loaded with every field into `fdi_projects` at every build. Its subject, `fdi_project`, accepts Public slots only. Every field of a project may appear on its card, in lists and on the map; no field is dropped at loading. Each card, list and map legend uses collapsed credits naming the agency as publisher, the data gatherer as the page names it, the date read and a link to the dashboard, with the page's own caveat: the data "includes estimates of publicly announced projects" and "does not capture all investment projects". Capital and jobs are always labelled as announced estimates. They are never added to measured figures, and are never shown beside them without that label ([decision 0026](decisions/0026-public-sources-used-in-full-locally.md) item 7). It is shown as a published announcement, never as a lookup of a firm, and is never joined to synthetic data, the attached records of a register company included (decision 0017; decision 0022).
- **Feed**: One delivery of one source contract for one reference period, produced by an adapter and checked by its conformance suite.
- **Feed bundle**: A feed manifest, coverage declaration and declared tables with hashes and row counts. Missing headers, scope or internal period fail acceptance.
- **Fixture (scene)**: A manifest-listed fixture identity accepted by the scene-only endpoint for a prepared “Try to break it” request. The complement step returns two pre-validated unit sets; production tools read a protected figure only after snapping an area to whole release units, and no run-time path holds unprotected data.
- **Fragment catalogue**: The versioned application-owned text fragments from which headline and decision templates may be selected. Model output chooses keys; it never edits the fragments.
- **Fidelity report**: The synthetic-world comparison with public margins and predeclared tolerances. It supports “consistent with published anchors within stated tolerances”, never “validated”.
- **Flow scale**: The positive fixed denominator of the first pack's flow index, `flow_score = 100 × sum(expanded_trips over situation hours) / flow_scale` (PRV-59), identical for A and B and never derived from protected-row sums. Version 1 releases flows as rounded estimates of trips, so it uses no flow scale; the rule applies only to an index that a contract keeps (decision 0016).
- **Forecast**: The output of tested code (method card C30) for one series of the closed, versioned list: a value with its empirical interval, its measured backtest error, its method and its source, as an Inferred fact on the subject and geography of its series. It is not a projection (a projection draws no interval), not a ranking and not a user's scenario, and it is never a forecast of the success of a venture. The same method card also forecasts the released monthly statistics of the custodian-type feeds (pay level, earner count and wage bill, the stayer index at municipality level, and employment by area): such a forecast carries the Synthetic class and the word "forecast", has the `area` of its series as subject and the last released month as origin, and is not made for a series with a carried-forward month (decision 0019 items 2 to 6). The establishments of an area by month are register companies by their own dates, a Public count rather than a released synthetic series; whether their forecast joins the closed list as a Public series is fixed with that list at the slice that builds card C30 (decision 0023 item 12; ARC-138).
- **Fuzz pairs**: Seeded random area pairs used as regression evidence after the exhaustive structural checks, not as the proof of closure; at least 10,000 pairs per release and per parameter set (PRV-49, PRV-51).

## G

- **Geofence**: See **Drawn area (geofence)**.
- **Greater Doha**: For version 1, the five whole municipalities of the frame's review file (task S2-T02; the lead's assumption, OI-151). Wherever the name appears on a screen or card, its definition appears with it, naming those five municipalities. Until that task merges, the pack refers to the review file instead of listing the names ([decision 0026](decisions/0026-public-sources-used-in-full-locally.md) item 10).
- **Grid cell**: One fixed H3 cell used for custodian-type statistics. Public statistics remain on official geographies.
- **Guided story**: The presenter journey of [16_DEMO_RUNBOOK.md](16_DEMO_RUNBOOK.md) from Public evidence through simulation, synthetic pay, privacy attacks and hand-over. Each of the four cases is also a guided story with a one-click start (decision 0016). It is designed for the three personas (see **Persona**), names no audience of a demonstration and keeps the presenter controls; its scene 7 is the international entrant's headquarters evidence pack (decision 0023 item 15).

## H

- **Hold-out**: Public evidence excluded from calibration and used later in the validation scorecard. It reduces circular checking but does not prove the simulated world is real.
- **Home family**: The single additive family a displayed figure is normally computed from, named in that fact's lineage (PRV-30). A count and adjacent non-ratio shares use the same home family. The exceptions to one home family per figure are the three of MTH-55: the closed ratio-statistic list, the one closed presence/pay case of DAT-160 (C32 and its use by C33), and the talent ratio (see **Ratio statistic**).
- **Housing class**: The housing-split values `family_housing`, `labour_accommodation`, `institution` and `unknown`, plus an explicit `other` row where pooling occurred. `other` is a treated leaf. The count that is not measured is a separate population, not a housing class.
- **How sure**: The sentence of method card C40 that says how often the **Best three sites** hold over the ranges the user set, "holds in X of 1,000 runs; most sensitive to ...", from seeded Monte Carlo runs and a Sobol design computed in code. It counts runs of the model and names the input with the largest total-order index, or, where no index is computed, says that no input stood out (MTH-114); it never states a percentage, a probability, a confidence level, a chance or a verdict, and it stands beside the rank-stability sentence wherever a rank is shown (decision 0023 item 5; MTH-115).

## I

- **Incentive package**: An incentive offer loaded from the national investment promotion agency's incentive pages into a structured record with its parameters and source page. It enters the break-even calculator only as an optional scenario input tagged "your inputs" and never changes a Public figure (decision 0017).
- **Income geography**: Area-level wage statistics for covered earners by home and workplace, released only as privacy-protected statistics. Decision 0016 names six indicators, each with trends: average and median wage by residential area where groups are large enough; wage bands and their spread; the covered wage bill and the earner count; residential income beside presence by time of day, with the people present by pay band of method card C32 as a third element, Inferred; residence and workplace geography; and wage trends by sector and area, separated from changes in worker mix (the trend by sector is a national Public figure from the wage series by economic activity, never a protected split). The average is never shown alone, and the figures describe covered earners and wages paid, not households, wealth or spending power.
- **Inferred**: Evidence status for a reasoned result, including a user scenario, a forecast and an estimate. It names its basis and, for catalogue claims, a permitted strength.
- **Input classes**: Every class that fed a composite fact, including Public, Synthetic, Simulated and, where applicable, user input.
- **International visitors**: The extra nationality group on movement aggregates, `international_visitors`, for visitors to the country. It is an aggregate like the other nationality groups, under the minimum-count rule, and it is not a person-level row and not a key that joins to a worker, residence or company record (decision 0023 item 16).
- **ISIC Rev.4**: The international activity classification used by the reviewed activity spine. Prefix similarity alone never approves a mapping.

## L

- **Leaf atom**: A mutually exclusive population and primitive inside one additive family: a unit, band, category, joint home-by-work category, side count or register category leaf. Only leaf atoms are perturbed. An `other` row is a treated leaf where pooling occurred. `ALL`, `all`, parent bands and municipality, national and area totals are derived inside their home family.
- **Layout**: The brief's ordered component instances, validated against the component catalogue before rendering. Unknown components, properties or fact kinds reject it.
- **Least-real rule**: Code that marks a composite Public only if all inputs are Public, otherwise Synthetic or Simulated according to the least-real input, with suppression taking precedence.
- **Ledger**: The append-only record of questions, tool calls, saved briefs and store switches, with release and seat context. It starts empty and holds only what live use writes, with no seeded history, and it is kept only as the internal **Question log**, with no screen (decision 0023 items 12 and 15; ARC-108).
- **Licence-activity count**: One activity on one commercial licence. It is not a firm, establishment or job.
- **Lineage**: The references that connect a fact to its release table, methods, sources, rules and release.
- **Live traffic layer**: The optional picture layer of live flow tiles, available only when `EII_TRAFFIC_PROVIDER` is `tomtom`, the key is present and the presenter's offline control is not set. The API process fetches the tiles and passes them on; the browser never holds the key. It is drawn with the provider's attribution visible while it is on, no numeral is read from a tile, and nothing of a response is stored (decision 0019 item 17). The default value is `simulated`.
- **Load receipt**: `load_receipt.json`, a loader output that is not listed in `manifest.artefacts`, with `state: complete` and the matching manifest `release_id`, written only after both stores are staged and verified. A protected-release receipt also carries the matching protected-window fields. Preflight and the API refuse a release without a complete, matching receipt.
- **Loader**: The only path from a release into either store. It verifies paths, hashes, schemas and allowed tables before atomically loading and writing the receipt.
- **Local ledger**: The SQLite fallback for ledger and egress rows while PostgreSQL is unavailable; the features that need PostgreSQL show “paused”, not an error, and the rows are replayed at a later start-up (OPS-21 to OPS-23). No screen shows the ledger (decision 0023 item 15).

## M

- **Manifest**: `manifest.json`, the release's declaration of `release_kind`, tables, artefacts, sources, applicable contracts, rules and digests. A `public_only` manifest omits the protected-window fields.
- **Map mode**: One of the three ways the map is drawn, behind one switch: the open map (the default, the only mode that needs no network, and the mode of the guided story, every test and every gate); satellite (the imagery provider's satellite imagery under the product's own layers, passed on by the local server with `ESRI_API_KEY`, with its credits inside the collapsed map credits); and photorealistic 3D (Google's photorealistic 3D tiles of its Map Tiles API, the one paid service of decision 0023, which the owner caps at USD 20 in total). For photorealistic 3D the product's own server adds `MAPTILE_API_KEY` to each request on a same-origin route, counts every billable request project-wide and refuses requests once the cap is reached; one view loads at most 600 tiles, the mode is never mixed with another basemap in one view, and it shows the attribution its tiles carry. Satellite and photorealistic 3D run online only and store no tile, the browser never holds either key, and no test or continuous integration run calls either provider. The satellite control is hidden when `ESRI_API_KEY` is absent (the lead's ruling on the owner's note). Other disabled states stay as they are: the offline control set, the network down, or, for photorealistic 3D, its key absent or the cap reached, each shown disabled with its reason. No figure is read from the imagery or the 3D tiles (decision 0023 item 7; FE-140, FE-141, FE-148). It is not the optional **Three-dimensional (3D) view**.
- **Method card**: The definition of one deterministic economic method, including inputs, formula, worked public example, provenance and permitted wording.
- **Mobility**: The record type for aggregate presence and movement. In this project it is simulated and contains no trajectories or device identifiers. Its aggregates carry the three splits of decision 0023 item 16 (see **Movement splits**).
- **Monthly period**: One month of the synthetic reference period, released inside the one protected window, whose protected `period` is the span of its months. Each month passes the same disclosure controls as a single period, one treatment holds for every month, and cross-month differencing attacks must recover nothing (decision 0016).
- **Movement splits**: The three one-way splits of the visitors of a movement cell, `age_band`, `gender` and `nationality_group`, as counts and shares. Presence splits count people present; flow splits count person trips, the feed's expanded trips before any conversion to vehicles, and are never a split of the vehicle-trip estimate of `flow_hourly`. A share is over the split's own total. No two splits are crossed, and none is crossed with the `segment` dimension. `gender` is `male` or `female`. `age_band` is `15-24`, `25-34`, `35-44`, `45-54`, `55-64` or `65+`. Visitors under 15 are counted in the age split's value `other`, with any pooled band; the split covers every visitor of the cell; a share is over the split's own total; no seventh band is added. In the age split `other` exists wherever a visitor under 15 is present, with or without a pooled band, and its label says "under 15 or pooled". The band edges are held in one configuration value (OI-148). `nationality_group` is the closed list of the worker records plus **International visitors**. Aggregates only: never a person-level row, and never a key that joins to a worker, residence or company record. Each value is a leaf under the movement family's own rules (the panel minimum, the rounding base, complementary suppression); values below the minimum are pooled into `other`, in the order and with the tie-break that PRV-35 gives for the labour splits, measured on the panel devices behind each value; if the pooled value is still below the minimum the smallest remaining value joins it, until it passes, else that split kind is withheld for the cell. A split kind with fewer than two values left beside `other` after pooling is withheld for the cell, with its reason (for `gender`, one value below the minimum withholds the split). A split is never shown as `other` alone. Each row of a split feed table carries `panel_devices`, as its parent table does. The simulator omits the rows of `presence_hourly` and `od_hourly` under 20 panel devices and records their withheld coverage in the coverage note before conformance, and that omission (SYN-57) and the row minimum of DAT-60 are rules of those two count tables: a presence cell is kept when all three segment rows of its group are in `presence_hourly`, and a group with a segment row omitted has no split rows; an origin-destination cell is kept when its `od_hourly` row is; a split feed table holds rows only for those kept cells and, within a kept cell, writes every split value with its count and `panel_devices`, the values below the minimum included, so that the values of each kind sum to the cell's total (presence, the three segments together; flows, the expanded trips) and a value omitted too early fails conformance; a split feed row never leaves the custodian plane; the panel minimum of a released split value is the movement family's panel minimum (PRV-58, `min_panel_devices`), measured on the panel devices behind it after the pooling of PRV-134, the feed rows pooled into `other` being below the minimum by construction; a feed table holds counts and `panel_devices` and never a share, shares being computed after the release build's pooling; and no release minimum, rounding base or pooling order changes. A released count is rounded with the family's base (`round_movement`). The values with `other` sum to the split's own total, so no withheld value and no remainder below the minimum can be recovered by subtraction, and a planted recoverable value fails (TST-260). Over several units, a split of an area is, per value, the sum of the published values of the area's snapped whole units; the units' `other` values sum into the area's `other`; a share is over the area's split total; the card says that `other` holds pooled values. A split is never apportioned to a drawn outline. A split's own total is never shown as the cell's presence figure and never set beside the segment counts as one figure (they are rounded separately); a split is shown as its values and their shares. DAT-127's test of `other` rows does not apply to the movement splits; DAT-169 says so. A split of visitors is never attributed to a named building or business (decision 0023 item 16).
- **Municipality**: An official public geography used for licences, population and coarse aggregates; it is not interchangeable with a showcase area.

Decision 0016 replaces it with **Estimated people present**. No presence figure is ever a named building's or business's footfall.

Public means what any visitor's browser receives: no sign-in, no paywall, no robot check passed, no back-end call with a key or token found in its pages, and read at a visitor's pace. Its fact lineage names both families. The closed list of exceptions to one home family per figure (PRV-30; MTH-55) is the mean wage and these shares, the one closed presence/pay case of DAT-160 (C32 and its use by C33), and the talent ratio of decision 0023 item 1 (see **Qualified people per competing job within N minutes**), and no other.

PRV-127 refuses any other ratio of two protected families and any ratio computed before release. The owner answered on 2026-11-14 (OI-140). Home-to-work flows are drawn as a flow map and animated as aggregate flows, and version 1 meets the case "totals between district pairs that pass the release minimums" by drawing each line from one whole home municipality of `ha_home` to the site's catchment snapped on `ha_work_count`, an exact sum of published `home_area_share` rows (card C39; OI-147), never as trails of individuals (SYN-59). These cases bind it and every other talent answer, among them pay benchmarks and the Qatarization planner: group statistics only, through the release rules (minimum counts of people and of employers in every cell, the dominance rule, and pay as bands, median and middle range); never a name, an identifier, a residential address, one person's pay or a person-level row; home locations no finer than the district; no employer named beside a talent pool or flow; and Qatari and non-Qatari splits only where the cell passes, otherwise withheld with its reason. It is computed by tested code from released rows of the attached labour and residence records under document 06 (method card C39; decision 0023 item 1, as the owner answered on 2026-11-14).
- **Target class**: People or employer groups the attacker-view model cannot tell apart, because they share the same published-unit memberships, the same contribution class and the same months. The audit solves each class once (PRV-112).
- **Target-surplus scenario**: An optional user scenario with target surplus share `U`, where `0 <= U < m`. Required revenue is `F / (m - U)` and breakpoints use `m - U`; `U = 0` is the default break-even case. A positive target is never called break-even and is not a revenue, demand or capture forecast.
- **Task ID**: The identifier and scope of one unit of work in [TASKS.md](../TASKS.md), used for its branch, review and evidence.
- **Template writer**: Deterministic code that composes the same structured brief from the same facts and catalogues without a model.
- **Three-dimensional (3D) view**: The optional view that extrudes whole release units by one protected area statistic, or by the simulated crowd density. It is off by default; every extruded value is also readable as a Fact chip in the flat view; marks, suppression and the snap rule are those of the flat view; a building keeps its own height and is never extruded by a data value; only traffic and crowd density animate (decision 0019 item 14).
- **Thirty-minute cap**: The rule that no drive-time catchment in the product goes beyond 30 minutes. The closed schema refuses a request above 30 minutes. The talent answer's 10, 20 and 30 are its own list, wherever the talent answer is given, which is every playbook whose bundle calls `workers_within_reach` (document 09 names them today: `staffing_and_pay` and `labour_market`). Every other drive or walk area keeps 5, 10 and 15 minutes. Drive 20 and drive 30 as area cut-offs exist only in the staffing playbook (decision 0023 item 17). The owner's words of 2026-11-14: "qatar is small 45 minutes is way too far".
- **Tier**: The count, money or quartile level at which a release unit and its primitives pass disclosure tests.
- **Tool status**: `OK`, `SUPPRESSED`, `NO_DATA` or `OUT_OF_SCOPE`; it is separate from provenance and evidence status.
- **Traffic assignment**: Tested code that assigns released hourly origin-destination flows to the road graph with one fixed volume-delay rule, giving Simulated congestion and link speeds by hour with the default traffic provider; drive-time areas change with the hour. Traffic is never computed from protected rows (decision 0016).
- **Traffic-aware drive-time area**: With the live provider on, an area of the session that the provider returns for a drive time. It enters through the adapter as the second entry route of a session area, is bound to the session and seat like a drawn shape, and is marked with the provider and its retrieval time. Figures over it follow the rules of a drawn shape and carry the least-real class of their inputs; the minutes are the user's own request and are refused above 30 (see **Thirty-minute cap**), and with the provider on a figure over a stored session area is repeatable for that area (decision 0019 item 17).
- **Trap**: A planted synthetic pattern that must be flagged or read cautiously, not interpreted naively.
- **Treatment**: Stable perturbation, rounding and band merging applied once at build time and stored, so repeated questions return identical answers.
- **Trust centre**: The view of rules, sources, licences, model boundaries, egress and limitations.
- **Typed-decision gateway**: `eii_gateway` in `gateway/`, an offline provider-neutral interface for bounded judgments over supplied inputs. Its default provider is `none`, and it never determines facts, numbers, privacy, gates or screen content.

## U

- **Unit member**: The mapping from a release unit to every base cell it contains, used to preserve exact partition membership.
- **User scenario**: A deterministic result from the user's own inputs, marked Inferred and “your inputs” and never stored in the release. The catchment-share scenario and the revenue range of the user's outlet (method cards C34 and C35) are user scenarios; where one reads a Simulated figure, it carries the Simulated mark by the least-real rule. A user scenario is never called a forecast.

## V

- **Validation scorecard**: The predeclared comparison of calibration anchors and separate hold-outs with fixed tolerances and reviewer evidence.
- **Vault**: The ephemeral custodian-plane folder for record-level synthetic data, deleted after every build and unreachable from the product.
- **Venue crosswalk**: The committed, reviewed table that maps each venue name seen in the public visitor-count series to one venue, because names vary between years. The build normalises names only through it and never edits a value by hand (decision 0017).
- **Viewing as badge**: The immutable display of the selected company seat (`company_a` or `company_b`), derived from its signed session context rather than editable interface state; it has no oversight state (decision 0023 item 15).

## W

- **Wage band**: One of the fixed B01 to B12 pay ranges; bands may be merged for privacy, and the top band remains open.
- **Wage bill**: An additive treated pay primitive used with earner counts to derive means and other statistics.
- **What changed**: Comparisons between released monthly periods, and of public series over time, computed by tested code: openings and closures of register companies, cluster change, and wage trends separated from changes in worker mix. It is Synthetic wherever its inputs are synthetic (decision 0016). Openings and closures are Public counts from the register's own dates and monthly snapshots, never generated and never a synthetic cohort (decision 0023 item 12; DAT-164).
- **Whole-cell sum**: The sum of the people of whole cells of the published grid `pub_pop_agesex_grid` over an area the user chose: a cell counts when its centre lies inside, and no cell is split. It is not an apportionment. It is a Public fact with the evidence status Observed, for display only, never a criterion of a ranking and never combined with, or charted with, a protected or Simulated figure (decision 0019 item 22; method card C38).
- **Window key**: The SHA-256 of canonical JSON holding the protected `period` and, for the four protected contracts (`business_register.v1`, `employment.v1`, `residence.v1`, `wages.v1`, in that name order), the pair `{name, sha256}` where `sha256` is the `feed_sha256` the manifest already records for that feed. It does not change when a Public or Simulated table, a scene fixture, a report or a treatment rule changes. A change that alters a feed under the same `world_seed` gives that seed a second `window_key`, which the uniqueness rule refuses; no invariance to scenario text is claimed (PRV-120).
- **Within reach**: The area made from a door by an allowed distance, walk or drive cut-off, by hour of day where congestion changes travel time; its protected figures snap to release units. No drive-time catchment goes beyond 30 minutes (see **Thirty-minute cap**). The talent answer's 10, 20 and 30 are its own list, wherever the talent answer is given; every other drive or walk area keeps 5, 10 and 15. Drive 20 and drive 30 as area cut-offs exist only in the staffing playbook (decision 0023 item 17).
- **Worked example**: A public or synthetic arithmetic example with a known answer, used as a regression test for a deterministic method.

## Z

- **Zone**: An official public geography used for utilities, census shares and momentum. It is never apportioned into protected grid facts.
