## Context

- On 2026-11-19 the owner asked that the map screen offer eight everyday capabilities that no task covers: a route between two points, a measuring tool, print and export of the current view and of the case file, a download of Public data, an imagery swipe, a share link, coordinates and go-to, and a phone layout of the map screen. The owner's request is the only reason given for this record (rules 15 and 24).
- TASKS.md was checked in full on 2026-11-19, capability by capability. Three of the eight already have queued coverage the new tasks reuse rather than repeat: S3-T16 already specifies an imagery swipe over the satellite and imagery-history views, S6-T39 already specifies the shareable URL state a link restores, and S6-T37 already specifies the one-handed phone bottom sheet and its size matrix. S7-T31, S7-T32 and S7-T34 therefore add the integration and acceptance work of the owner's request on top of those implementations and defer or replace no earlier acceptance criterion of them; no existing task covers the other five capabilities or the integration each of the three adds. The nearest work is named under each item as a dependency, never a duplicate: the routing graph and the travel-time machinery of S6-T13 and BE-119 for the route; the calculation layer of S2-T07 and the drawing tools of S3-T13 for the measure; the printable pack renderer of S10-T05 and the case machinery of slices S8 and S9 for print and export; the living map's Public layers of S3-T12 and the Fact chip and source chain of S3-T09 for the download; the map modes, the satellite mode and the before-and-after swipe of S3-T16 over the imagery-history views of S2-T19 for the imagery swipe; the saved views and shareable URL state of S6-T39 for the share link; the bilingual gazetteer of S2-T02 and `resolve` of S3-T03 for go-to; and decision 0049's one responsive layout, its eight named sizes and the Explore screen of S6-T37 for the phone layout.
- Numbering checked on 2026-11-19: the last task number in use is S7-T27 in slice S7 and S10-T07 in slice S10, so under the queue rule (a task added by a re-plan takes the next free number of its slice and is listed where it runs; a task ID is never reused or renumbered) the new tasks are S7-T28 to S7-T34 and S10-T08.
- Slice choice. Seven of the eight capabilities extend the map screen as slices S1 to S6 leave it: every one of their dependencies (S2-T02, S2-T07, S2-T19, S3-T03, S3-T09, S3-T12, S3-T13, S3-T16, S3-T19, S6-T08, S6-T13, S6-T37, S6-T39) is merged by slice S7, the slice the plan gives to work beyond the flagship — the same home the queue already gives the living-map cards S7-T23 and S7-T24. Putting any of them in slice S6 would hold the flagship gate for work that is not the flagship. Print and export lands in slice S10 beside the printable pack renderer of S10-T05 that it reuses, because the case file exists only after slice S8 builds the cases and S9-T02 binds the sample files to their seat.
- Two of the tasks compute numbers (S7-T28, S7-T29): rules 16, 17 and 23 bind them, so every figure is returned by tested code in `eii_core.calc` and appears only through the Fact chip family. Three of the tasks move values to an outside surface (S7-T30, S7-T32, S10-T08): rules 8 and 24 bind them, so no Synthetic or Simulated fact, and no estimate, share or score, about a named real place enters an export, a download or a share link, and no hidden or protected value enters one at all.

## Decision

Accepted, 2026-11-19. Items 1 to 8 add one task each; item 9 states what does not change.

1. **A route between two points — new task S7-T28 of slice S7.** The user picks two points on the map and a route is drawn between them, with its length and its drive time returned by tested code in `eii_core.calc` over the routing graph artefact, `road_link` and `traffic_hourly` that `travel_times` and `reachable_access` already read (BE-119): road data the pack already has, or a further registered Public road source with its licence (rule 5). The drive time uses the released speeds by hour and carries the Simulated class wherever simulated speeds enter, exactly as the drive-time figures of S6-T13 and the gateway table do. No live traffic is needed: decision 0019 item 17 is unchanged, and no test, gate, command or the guided story needs the provider, a key or the network (rules 4 and 27). Every figure is a Fact chip; a route no path joins or that leaves the mapped land area returns `NO_DATA` with its reason.
2. **A measuring tool — new task S7-T29 of slice S7.** Distance along a drawn line and area of a drawn shape, computed by tested code in `eii_core.calc` (rules 16 and 17) and shown only through the Fact chip family (rule 23), drawable by pointer, touch and keyboard under decision 0042 item 7. The drawn shape keeps the existing polygon ingress of BE-117 and FE-108, which is not broadened; the drawn line enters through a separate closed, typed user-line input contract that S7-T29 owns, its route and schema written into document 08 section 8.3 and document 03 section 9.2 before the task becomes active, bound to the session and seat as a drawn shape is, refusing malformed input and extra properties and never written by a model. A measure is the user's own figure over the base map with subject `area` and evidence status Inferred; it is never an attribute of a named real place (rule 8).
3. **Print and export of the current view and of the case file — new task S10-T08 of slice S10.** A print and export layout in which every figure is a Fact chip whose complete source chain appears in the export's source section and every map layer drawn carries its attribution (BRF-19, FE-38, TST-100, TST-103; the layer credits of PUB-110). It reuses the pack renderer and print styles of S10-T05. A case file's export is produced only inside the session and seat that loaded it (BRF-80, BE-109); no protected value enters an export and a suppressed or withheld fact exports nothing (PRV-85), a planted released protected area aggregate and a planted fact of each prohibited named-entity category — a Synthetic or Simulated fact about a named real place, building or business, and an estimate, share or score for one — each refused and failing (rules 8 and 24); and no audience origin label appears while method honesty and source access remain (decision 0035; DES-78).
4. **A download of Public data shown on screen — new task S7-T30 of slice S7.** The Public rows behind a layer or table download with the source, licence and retrieval date of each row (rule 5; PUB-12). No protected value enters a download, and a Synthetic, Simulated or protected value in one is refused, planted and failing, a released protected area aggregate among the plants; a suppressed or withheld fact downloads nothing (PRV-85). No Synthetic or Simulated fact, and no estimate, share or score, attributed to a named real place, building or business enters a download even on a Public row, and a planted row of each of those categories fails, a Public estimate on a named real place among them (rules 8 and 24). Filenames, column headings and export text carry no audience origin label under the standing check of S3-T19 (decision 0035).
5. **An imagery swipe — new task S7-T31 of slice S7.** Two imagery layers side by side with a moving divider, each layer's attribution and date visible while it is on (PUB-110). The compared layers are the imagery the pack already serves: the satellite mode of S3-T16 where its key rules allow it, and the imagery-history views of S2-T19. The swipe keeps a baseline visible with an immediate reset, has a single-pointer non-drag and a keyboard alternative (decision 0042 item 7; FE-80) and stops under reduced motion. The provider rules are unchanged: no tile is stored, the browser holds no key, and no test, gate, command or the guided story needs a provider or a key (OPS-73, TST-248; rules 4 and 27).
6. **A share link — new task S7-T32 of slice S7.** A link that restores the view, layers and area, built on the shareable URL state of S6-T39: a closed state schema of authorised view, layer and selection inputs only that rejects fact values and unknown properties, carrying only what its rules permit — no personal data, no visitor-typed text, no hidden count, no protected-shape vertices, no protected value, no Synthetic or Simulated fact and no estimate, share or score attributed to a named real place, building or business, and no sample rows, a planted value of each prohibited category in the state failing; nothing is stored server-side beyond what the application already keeps (BE-109). A restore passes the same seat and release checks as any entry, its permitted identifiers resolved under the receiving session's seat and release, and a drawn area that the permitted state cannot encode is not restored, with the reason shown.
7. **Coordinates and go-to — new task S7-T33 of slice S7.** The pointer position shows as coordinates — each a returned fact object produced by deterministic tested code or a documented, tested fixed application-schema read function, every precision conversion and rounding done by that code and every coordinate numeral rendered through the Fact chip family (rules 16, 17 and 23), with planted failures for a raw geometry-derived readout, a browser rounding and a coordinate outside a Fact chip — and a jump flies the map to typed coordinates or to a known area name resolved through the bilingual gazetteer of S2-T02 and the `resolve` path of S3-T03. Typed coordinates enter only through a closed schema (rule 18); the go-to works with no network and inside the frozen bundle; the live address search of FE-142 is unchanged; and typed text goes nowhere but the local resolver.
8. **A phone layout of the map screen — new task S7-T34 of slice S7.** The same map content and rules at phone width, in both languages, under decision 0049's one layout that responds to the screen: at the two phone sizes of its item 2, in portrait and landscape, every map-screen control is reachable one-handed, nothing overflows or hides the map's answer card (FE-69), targets meet their required sizes (FE-73), and the map answers first (item 4). The visual tests of TST-90 cover it.
9. **Nothing else changes.** The eight tasks joined the queue as `queued` when this record was confirmed. No requirement ID, gate, method card, privacy rule, catalogue, command or existing task is amended, and decision 0019 item 17 stands unchanged. Where a part of a new task has no requirement ID yet, this record itself proposes the obligation: the task identifies the missing contract or method requirement by document and section and obtains the defined ID before the task becomes active — a provision of this record that keeps PLN-26's requirement-coverage principle, not a claim that PLN-26 already provides it. Rule 42 applies wherever a task uses a library newer than most training data, and its brief names the pages read (decision 0054).
