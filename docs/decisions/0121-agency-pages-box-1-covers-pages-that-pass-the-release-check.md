## Decision

1. S2-T39 box 1 reads: "every Arabic page that passes the release check of
   decision 0029 item 5 is quoted in full and unchanged". The Arabic pages

   visible, and each language shows the agency's own text.
2. Pages refused by that release check are listed in OI-414 with their
   reasons. Their typed records and links remain, with the missing-page
   quotation state of decision 0029 item 4. No text is redacted or exempted
   from the contact check.
3. The two flags `field_coverage_recorded` and `live_page_compared` read over
   the stored pages, meaning the pages with a `pub_investqa_page` row after
   the release check. Refused pages remain explicitly accounted for in
   OI-414. Inventory and fixture-comparison requirements remain unchanged.
4. [Decision 0120](0120-year-range-is-not-a-phone-shape.md) proposes only the
   exact year-range reading. The lead reruns the real evidence, records
   passing and refused pages, and then updates the two flags and ticks box 1
   only when that rerun proves its requirements. This workspace change
   leaves both flags false and the acceptance box unticked.
