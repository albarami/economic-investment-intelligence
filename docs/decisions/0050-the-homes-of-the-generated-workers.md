## Context

Task S4-T03 generates the wages and residence of the workers attached to the register companies ([decision 0022](0022-real-register-companies-with-a-synthetic-world.md) item 2), and its first acceptance criterion is that the full month window conforms. The residence contract's conformance suite refuses a feed whose `unknown` housing-class share reaches 2% (DAT-54, one of the proposed acceptance thresholds named in DAT-24). The manifest entry that drives that share, `pop.residence_not_measured` of document 05 section 3.1 (SYN-33), holds the default 3%, the range 1% to 8% and the sweep 1/3/8. At that default the generated feed's `unknown` share sits at or above DAT-54's threshold, the suite refuses the feed, and the criterion cannot hold. The plan's remedy, ruled on by the lead (ruling 7 (a)), moves the parameter's default to the lowest value of its own sweep and leaves the suite's threshold untouched, with a decision record for the slice gate.

The same plan fixes whose homes are built: residence rows for the persons of the employment feed only, with the share of workers in labour accommodation by occupation group as one new ASSUMED manifest entry with range and sweep (ruling 8). The design review's finding F1, adopted by the lead, makes that entry a material amendment of document 05 section 3.1: it needs a decision record proposed with the task, drafted together with the record of ruling 7, and reviewed by another maker before the homes are built. This is that record, written under AGENTS.md rule 46. It changes documents only, and it claims no built generator and no passed gate.

## Decision items

1. **The default of `pop.residence_not_measured` becomes the lowest value of its own sweep.** The default moves from 3% to 1%; the range (1% to 8%) and the sweep (1/3/8) are unchanged, and no sweep value is added. A feed built at the new default carries an `unknown` share under DAT-54's threshold, so the full month window can conform. DAT-54 is not touched: neither its `unknown` threshold (under 2%) nor its resolved-share clause (at least 97%) changes, and a later change of the threshold moves only through a decision record, never to pass a run (AGENTS.md rule 12). Of the existing sweep values, 3% and 8% are at or above the threshold: a sweep run at one of them builds a feed the residence suite refuses, and the run is recorded as refused in the sweep report, never passed by loosening the suite, dropping the run or editing DAT-54. The change drives SYN-33, the `unknown` housing class with its own `unknown` row in the housing split (PRV-39), whose rules do not change. The contradiction the plan found at the old default stays open at the sweep values, and with the real records' own `unknown` share unknown: open item OI-[LEAD-1].

2. **A new ASSUMED entry family: the share of workers in labour accommodation by occupation group.** Residence rows are generated for the persons of the employment feed only (ruling 8): the covered private-sector spells of document 03 section 6.2, the persons `residence.v1` already declares. No residence row is generated for anyone else, and no person, record or attribute is added (AGENTS.md rule 3; [decision 0023](0023-ten-additions-and-the-data-rule.md) item 12). The share of each occupation group's workers whose residence row carries the housing class `labour_accommodation` enters document 05 section 3.1 as ASSUMED entries with default, range and sweep. Ruling 8 names one entry; the manifest's schema admits exactly one scalar parameter per entry (SYN-06, SYN-69), so the entry is one key family with one row per occupation group, as `emp.deployed_share.*` already is: `res.labour_accommodation_share.1` to `res.labour_accommodation_share.9` and `res.labour_accommodation_share.unmapped`, the groups being the ISCO-08 major groups of DAT-127 plus `unmapped`. No entry exists for group `0`, which the coverage exclusions of `pop.coverage_exclusions` keep out of the feed; SYN-07 already stops a generator that asks for an absent key.

   The proposed values are this record's ASSUMPTION: defaults by group `1` 15%, `2` 15%, `3` 25%, `4` 15%, `5` 30%, `6` 40%, `7` 65%, `8` 65%, `9` 75%, `unmapped` 40%; one range for every group, 0% to 90%; one sweep per group, its low end, its default and its high end. The reasoning, drawn only on Public facts already in the pack, in each source's own categories and geography and never apportioned to a drawn area (AGENTS.md rule 10):

   - No Public source in the pack splits labour-accommodation residence by occupation group. The census-night household-type table counts labour camps, households, and public housing and institutions on its own household-type categories and municipal geography, December 2020 [S5]; the census zone layers hold the employed by occupation per zone, 2020, with no housing type [S6]; the wage tables hold means by occupation [S11]; no private calibration table holds it either. So no Public fact supports any per-group value, and every default, range end and sweep end of the family is an ASSUMPTION: open item OI-[LEAD-2], "labour-accommodation share by occupation group (data owner)".
   - The shape of the defaults is engineering judgement on what the pack already holds: the statutory minimum wage is a basic with housing and food provided in kind or paid on top (Law No. 17 of 2020 [S10]); the pack already treats labour-accommodation workers as provided with housing and food (document 05, ASSUMPTION 6); and the Public mean wages by occupation group [S11] place managers, professionals and clerical workers far above that floor and elementary occupations, craft workers and machine operators at it, where `wage.heap_at_minimum` heaps the lowest draws. The defaults rise from the managerial and professional groups to the elementary group along that pay ordering, and the middle `unmapped` default reflects free-text titles across the range.
   - The level is not anchored and is not a claim. The one Public figure that touches the level is the census-night labour-camp share of the enumerated population, 46.6% in 2020 and 60.0% in 2015 [S5], national; it describes the whole census-night population, not the covered workforce, and it stays where it is, the anchor of the people table's housing classes. The range is one wide band (0% to 90%) for every group because no Public fact bounds any group more narrowly; its top end is this record's choice, short of the degenerate case in which a group's every worker is at an accommodation site.
   - The entries replace no anchored value and set no anchored total: `pop.housing_split`, `pop.housing_split_drift` and `pop.occupation_by_zone` stay ANCHORED, F3 stays exact and F7 keeps its tolerance; where an anchored margin and one of these entries speak to the same total, the anchored margin governs, and no anchored value is re-drawn to fit an ASSUMED entry (SYN-83's precedence; AGENTS.md rule 12).

3. **The generator rows each change drives, and the tests that must exist.** The default change of item 1 drives SYN-33 (the `unknown` housing-class share and its `unknown` row); the entry family of item 2 drives SYN-31 (which workers are housed in labour accommodation with their employer, at sites holding staff of 1 to `res.employers_per_accommodation` employer groups) and, through the complement it leaves, the family-housing pool that SYN-32 sorts. Task S4-T03 must carry both tests, each with a planted violation (AGENTS.md rule 37): a planted feed whose `unknown` housing-class share reaches DAT-54's threshold is still refused by the residence suite, and a suite that passes it fails; and the generator reads the `res.labour_accommodation_share.*` entries only from the calibration manifest, so a planted generator that reads them from anywhere else, or holds one of their values as a literal, fails (SYN-02, SYN-06, SYN-07, SYN-69).

4. **What does not change.** No gender, sex or nationality attribute exists on a residence row (AGENTS.md rule 10; the `residence` table of document 03 section 6.4 has none; DAT-141; F6). No Synthetic or Simulated fact is attached to a named place or business (AGENTS.md rule 8): a residence row names no place, only a base cell (DAT-50), and the entries are shares of invented persons attached to register companies on decision 0022's terms. The `unknown` class keeps its separate value, its own row in the housing split and the PRV-39 display rules. No privacy rule, minimum count, tolerance, check, attack size or suite threshold changes (AGENTS.md rule 12), and F14 stays exact at every sweep value of the new entries, including the 90% end.

## Consequences

- Task S4-T03's homes part builds on this record: the TASKS.md amendment line carries the two tests of item 3. This record is reviewed by another maker before the homes are built (finding F1); the reviewer edits nothing and returns APPROVED or REJECTED with file, line, defect and exact fix (AGENTS.md rule 35).
- The sensitivity sweep of `pop.residence_not_measured` now holds a default that conforms and two values whose feeds are refused; the sweep report records those runs as refused, and the refusal is the recorded outcome, not a failure of the sweep machinery and never a reason to touch DAT-54.
- The values of the new entry family are this record's ASSUMPTION, not calibration; a later default, range or sweep change of the family goes through the manifest review the pack already fixes, and a value supplied later by the owner as an aggregate calibration table joins the world build on decision 0022's and decision 0036's terms, replacing the ASSUMPTION it covers.
- The lead numbers the two open items OI-[LEAD-1] and OI-[LEAD-2].
- This record claims no implementation: no generator, feed, suite or gate is asserted to exist or to pass.
