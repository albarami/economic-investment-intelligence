## Context

The owner's relayed note asks for useful, easy-to-use, visually strong decision workflows. L-674 items 2 and 3 adopt a foundation while preserving decision 0035: the research's audience origin words and origin textures are not adopted. Source chains, true Public attribution, method limitations and withheld states remain. Document 10, Stack and pins, previously excluded a chart library; document 11, DES-11 and DES-136, specified two levels of depth. These are amendments, not newly discovered requirements. The research and its thresholds are proposals, not measurements in this application (L-674 item 1; decision 0038 item 7).

The further Explore note and conversational-map note of 2026-11-16 are adopted by L-677: free inspection becomes a first-class screen beside the story, and location questions receive a map-first answer with the map also supplying question context. The existing foundations are document 10 section 3.6 and Shell and session (Reset), document 09 AI-101 and AI-46, and decision 0038 Accepted spike inventory item 8. L-677 places the work after the S5 release in S6 and with the assistant in S8. Its ruling overrides the Explore note's proposed provenance class on lenses: decision 0035 permits no audience origin word, class, texture or label.

The owner golden-scenario note (NOTE.golden.md) and L-679 make the Lusail Italian restaurant conversation the golden scenario of Explore and the conversational map, extending the flagship of document 01 section 7 (D2). L-679 replaces the note's suggested advice with a tested, visible-criteria ranking: presence stays on area units, and the best-fit view follows the evidence by default rather than waiting for where else.

## Decision

1. **Component layer.** Use React Aria beneath EII's own components, with one overlay and focus system. Shadcn-style owned components may be a starting point. A second primitive library requires a recorded need; this is no permission to bypass the closed component catalogue (document 09, AI-46).
2. **Charts.** ECharts is first, with selective imports, ARIA descriptions, decal textures and an accessible table for every chart. Decals may distinguish series, never data origin (decision 0035 items 1 and 6). One canonical Western-digit formatter is shared by both languages inside the Fact chip family; every numeral, including axes, tooltips, tables and accessible descriptions, stays under rule 23 and FE-39 to FE-45. Observable Plot and visx may be adopted only if S6-T34 passes them. Vega-Lite is not a runtime: another general grammar and interaction system is unnecessary here. No model-authored chart specification, expression or data address reaches a renderer (rules 16 and 21 to 23).
3. **Motion.** Use Motion with LazyMotion, respecting reduced motion and the existing design tokens (DES-48 and DES-51). No number animates through invented intermediate values (DES-168). Camera moves are interruptible in story and exploration, amending DES-49's ignored-input clause.
4. **Catalogue protocol.** EII's own loader gains catalogue version negotiation, severity-tagged validation results, and a user-activation flag on keys with side effects, borrowing A2UI's vocabulary. Unsupported versions, unknown keys and properties, wrong fact slots and unactivated side effects are rejected. Severity cannot downgrade a blocking failure. Document 09, AI-35 to AI-50, still requires complete validation, one repair and then template fallback; interrupted streams expose no partially validated claim. No A2UI runtime enters the critical path. One validated action dispatcher serves story, Explore and assistant; S6-T33 tests that shared boundary and decides whether an adapter is worth retaining (L-677).
5. **Assistant transport.** AG-UI is at most a thin adapter, compared with plain application events in S6-T33 by lines of code and failure modes. The CopilotKit platform is not taken: its Python runtime requires its paid Intelligence platform, as reported by the supplied research under its item 9, not checked here. Transport cannot supply facts, prose or authority beyond the closed contracts (AI-46 and AI-50).
6. **Skipped options.** Skip the OpenUI Lang runtime because its additional language and execution surface do not justify independent enforcement work; MCP Apps because embedding inside another assistant is not the present distribution target (reconsider for that distribution); Tambo because the hosted service closed on 31 October 2026, as reported by the research, leaving a maintenance transition; json-render as a dependency because its value must first be demonstrated by S6-T33; and Flutter because the reported genui migration gives no reason to move the existing React application. These are dependency decisions, not comparisons with existing commercial products.
7. **Design amendments.** Use decision-first entry and three disclosure levels: answer, comparison, evidence and method. Every numerical view resolves to one fact object; withheld results show their reason, never zero (FE-41, FE-75; DES-75). The first screen is a quiet map, one Public evidence layer with its dated Fact chip, the question "What decision are you making?", visible site choice, suppliers, talent, competition and forecasts actions, and Guided story beside Explore. These five entry actions do not remove the seven journeys of DES-183; Public here is a backend class, not a visible word. Phones require an inspectable bottom sheet and persistent selection summary: this supersedes the phone exclusion in document 10, Layouts, and DES-01, but the gap check classifies its implementation as MISSING-COSTLY; the lead assigns that work without adding a critical-path dependency. Add the privacy boundary in scene 6 (a withheld small-area result offering only approved broader aggregations, never its hidden count) and the scenario fork in scene 5 (baseline stays visible during an assumption change, immediate reset; existing scenario eligibility remains). Every drag has a single-pointer non-drag alternative, including named-area choice and click-to-place vertices for drawing. Keep the MapLibre 6 right-to-left text plugin until the pinned minor version is demonstrated not to need it, superseding the earlier built-in-shaping assumption (document 10, Stack and pins; DES-56). All these changes obey decision 0035 items 1 to 4.
8. **Pins.** Each library gets an exact pin, retreat pin and reference note in the task that first imports it, after reading current official documentation (rule 42; FE-01 and FE-02). This record installs nothing and establishes no compatibility, licence clearance or performance result.
9. **Spikes.** Add S6-T33 to S6-T36 off the critical path, on idle seats, starting after this record merges (L-674 items 4 and 7). Each writes its pass/fail contract before running and plants a violation for every guard. Proposed thresholds are measured and reported, never loosened after a run. A failed or blocked spike records the reason and retains the baseline. The owner's native traces follow S1-T12 and never hold up the build. These four tasks do not enter the blanket dependencies of S6-T06 or block the existing S6 gate; their own checks and different-maker review remain mandatory. OI-257 asks the owner to recruit 12 to 16 users balanced by working language once the flagship journey runs, with proposed SUS score at least 80 and at least 90 per cent correct reading of source chains and withheld results; the build never waits for recruitment.
10. **Explore screen.** Build a first-class screen beside Guided story, usable without a journey: select a place, area, register company or layer value to open an inspector with Fact chips, a reviewed-template explanation and computed comparison against Qatar or the nearest valid benchmark, and buttons to compare, draw an area, ask or open the evidence thread. S6-T37 owns the screen, meaningful opening evidence, contextual hints and designed empty, loading, withheld and offline states, with a one-handed phone bottom sheet, persistent selection summary and tap-and-hold inspection; S6-T38 owns bilingual place, street, activity and register-company search with transliteration and typo tolerance under decision 0038's entity resolution (S6-T31), retaining uncertain candidates rather than merging them; S6-T39 owns named lenses with legends, stacking limits, a compare tray for two or three selections on identical scales, saved views, shareable state, undo/redo and guided-path/return-to-story controls. Each lens says what it shows and does not show and reaches sources through the evidence thread, never an origin word, class, texture or label (decision 0035; L-677). Every displayed value obeys the release, suppression, subject and Fact-chip rules; an unsupported building or company value is withheld, never invented or recovered from a hidden count (PRV-18, PRV-24, PRV-83 to PRV-86; AI-42). S6-T37 measures the proposed two-interaction first inspection and panel updates under 100 ms at the 95th percentile, with smooth panning, bilingual identical fact strings and frozen-bundle offline operation; targets are fixed before measurement and never loosened after a run. All story evidence must be reachable by hand: the slice S10 gate adds reproduction of each impact moment of document 11 in Explore; S10-T06 remains review follow-up only. Request-limit coverage is OI-258; PRV-89 already covers area requests as an operational control, not a privacy guarantee. OI-259 schedules the Explore remote check under S3-T20 once both exist, without editing S3-T20 now (Explore note, Requirements and Acceptance; L-677).
11. **Conversational map.** S8-T15 makes the first visible answer to a location, area, company or layer question a map action, before the first reviewed-template sentence completes and alongside Fact chips; it retains the resulting map state and an answer trail with back, undo and interruption. Anchored callouts use Fact chips and one reviewed-template sentence; numbered candidate pins use returned rank facts through chips in the same order as the ranking panel, with its criteria and rank-stability sentence, and show the supporting rings, reach or cells. Extend the closed directive list with typed candidates, callout, compare, reach, focus and restore actions: ids, enumeration keys and authorised coordinates only, never a number to render, query, model-written prose or protected-shape vertices; the existing ban on directive shape vertices and weights remains (document 10 section 3.6). An unknown or malformed directive is ignored and logged at dispatch; AI-46's brief rejection, one repair and template fallback and AI-50's complete-brief accounting remain. Map-first is presentation order after validation, never partially validated streaming. S8-T16 makes a touched selection, drawn shape, radius or selected company the next question's context with hour, layers and language; the model receives authorised identifiers, not invented geometry. Ambiguity uses candidate pins to tap; follow-up buttons and the short “what can I ask here” list are valid catalogue actions. Dragging a radius, hour or weight handle re-runs only closed tools on typed user input, with non-drag alternatives. A short “show me” flight has reviewed captions, optional existing voice and immediate stop. Measure the proposed one-interaction ask and dragged-handle answer update under 100 ms at the 95th percentile; fix the protocol before the run and never loosen a target afterwards. Both languages, phone, offline frozen-bundle behaviour and eventual S3-T20 remote mode keep the same release, suppression, subject and Fact-chip controls with no recoverable hidden count. S8-T10 retains transcript confirmation and template-only speech; speech models remain optional. These tasks use item 4's shared dispatcher and S6-T33's adversarial fixtures, with a planted violation for every guard (conversational-map note, Requirements and Acceptance; L-677).
12. **The golden scenario.** The Italian restaurant in Lusail is the golden scenario of the conversational map and Explore, built on document 01 section 7 (D2), never replacing it (owner golden-scenario note, NOTE.golden.md; L-679 points 1 to 5). Document 10 carries the scene-by-scene storyboard and acceptance. S8-T15 puts the map on the resolved place before the first sentence ends, with a place-at-a-glance card of the district's Public facts only and real activity businesses as pins with density from Public places data and the incompleteness note during the reply. After the evidence, best-fit candidate areas appear by default as the tested ranking tool's output over visible, editable competition density, presence at the chosen hour on area units and access criteria, with weights and the rank-stability sentence; where else remains available. Never display recommended wording, advice, a verdict or a probability; no presence figure belongs to a named place (rules 8, 24 and 25). S8-T17 supplies closed, reviewed, versioned question sets per playbook family in the question catalogue, driven by the activity spine: chips carry catalogue reasons, answers are closed enumeration values changing weights or filters as typed inputs, and each answer visibly re-ranks through an interruptible transition with catalogue text. Skip, undo and reset work; no model-written question or free text enters this path (rules 18, 21 and 22). At least three clarifying questions change the ranking visibly and reversibly. Prove the flow for three activities from document 09 section 3, restaurant first; S8-T17 uses pharmacy and industrial maintenance services for the other two. Every number is a Fact chip, decision 0035 permits no audience origin word or mark, and item 11's complete validation remains. Every guard has a planted violation, including named-place presence, model-written question, ranking unequal to the tool output, recommended wording, free text and unknown enumeration value. The slice S10 gate gains the end-to-end golden scenario in Arabic and English on the stage laptop and a phone, with two further activities.
13. **Unchanged controls.** No gate, check, privacy rule, attack size or tolerance is weakened. Closed catalogues and application-owned wording under rule 21, Fact chips under rule 23, deterministic calculations, complete source chains, and decision 0035 remain binding. This documents-only decision claims no implementation or gate pass (rule 47).

## Evidence and consequences

Authority is the owner's original relayed note of 2026-11-16 and L-674, especially items 2 to 7, and the two further Explore and conversational-map notes adopted by L-677; the ruling wins over the research. Library capabilities and licences below are reported by that research, whose access date is 16 November 2026, and were not independently checked in this offline documentation pass. No library was installed or measured. Thresholds and usability targets are proposals. ECharts adapters must preserve the existing chip boundary instead of enabling library-generated numeric text. The cheap design fold-ins are recorded below; phone layout implementation is new work, and proposed criteria for existing S3 tasks are returned to the lead without editing those entries.

The two further notes add delivery work, not measured performance: S6-T37 to S6-T39 follow the release, S8-T15 and S8-T16 extend the assistant, and the S10 gate evidence is strengthened. PRV-49 to PRV-51, PRV-71 and PRV-76 retain their differencing and attack coverage; PRV-89 supplies an existing area-request limit. Neither a faster interaction nor a rate limit replaces disclosure control (L-677; document 06, Run-time behaviour and the privacy receipt).

The owner golden-scenario note and L-679 add S8-T17 and strengthen S8-T15 and the slice S10 gate, without changing any check or limit. The storyboard and task criteria are future acceptance evidence, not measured behaviour; the map-first sequence still follows AI-50. The placement correction restores S10-T06's original review-only acceptance and moves Explore reproduction into the gate paragraph.
