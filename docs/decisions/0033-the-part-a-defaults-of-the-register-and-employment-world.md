## Context

The lead builds task S4-T02 in two parts. Part A builds the December 2025 employment stock and one month's register snapshot on the real register files; Part B builds the months after it and fills what Part A leaves open. The split is the lead's plan of the task, not a change of its entry in TASKS.md. Document 05 leaves six questions to this task; the build brief left their defaults, the table's two format values and the sandbox roles to a ruling. On 2026-11-15 the lead read the structure of the real files in its own shell — counts, key sets, forms and yes-or-no answers only — and ruled. Items 1 to 8 state the rulings; item 9 says what this pass does not prove.

## Decision

The lead's decision under the owner's delegation, for the owner's confirmation. Items marked "by default" hold until the owner says otherwise, each under its named open item.

1. **Q13 — establishments and legal entities.** The commercial file shows no main-and-branch structure: `branches` is empty on every row and `establishment_type` (8 distinct text values) says nothing of one, so decision 0023 item 12 applies in its simplest form. One register row is one establishment; one legal entity stands for each distinct `registration_number`. By default, until the owner says otherwise: a row without a registration number is counted and left out of the snapshot and of the stock, with its reason, because section 6.1 makes the number a field that is never null and the key by which the attached records join their company (decision 0022 item 2; DAT-12); the commercial file as read on 2026-11-15 holds 1,458 such rows. Open item: whether such a company is keyed by another number the register publishes. The number is normalised under DAT-02 (trim, upper-case, remove inner spaces and non-breaking spaces, and convert Arabic-Indic digits to ASCII) and validated against the register file under DAT-12; it is never parsed as a quantity and never matched against a synthetic-identifier length or pattern. Within Part A the number is written to the `registration_number` column of the feed in the vault and to no report, log, message, coverage file or repository file (decision 0022 item 8); its later handling in the release and serving stores is governed by section 6.1's LIST crossing and DAT-15. Every other identifier of the feed is the synthetic one. Hidden groups and generated multi-establishment entities exist only in the invented stand-in register of the tests (decision 0023 item 12).
2. **Q14 — the activity.** The register publishes no code list and no code column, so `activity_code` is null for every company (document 03 section 6.1 `establishment_activity`: "null when only text exists"); `activity_text` is `business_category` as published, `business_type` where the category is empty, and a company with neither is counted and left out of the snapshot with its reason, as a company without a name is. A company's one activity row is its primary row (DAT-23); `classification` is not an activity and is not read. A company's ISIC section comes only through the reviewed activity map, keyed by the normalised activity text — never by a model or a keyword (DAT-22: only through the reviewed `activity_map`) and never by a prefix (DAT-24). The code fields stay null until a register file shows a code. DAT-24's code-distribution check is NOT PROVED for this code-free file; that is an interim Part A state, not a reading DAT-24 allows as final. Open item: a later decision must establish how the unchanged DAT-24 is proved before a finished feed or release. Open item: the review, by a person, of the 78 category texts and 8 type texts to ISIC sections — the owner's step, needed before the first real world build can place a company in an activity cell of the table.
3. **Q15 — "dormant".** `emp.dormant_share` (document 05 section 3.1; SYN-24) is unchanged and applies to the December-eligible companies only; a company drawn dormant has no jobs.
4. **Q17 — the workers' nationality groups, by default.** By default, until the owner says otherwise: no share value is committed in any file; the group keys reach the build only through the owner's file named by `EII_NATIONALITY_GROUPS_FILE`, read by key and SHA-256; a share the build needs that no approved specification gives refuses with `missing_calibration`; a committed share must be an ASSUMED manifest entry (SYN-06) with no real origin and no origin in a Public split (SYN-13). Open item: the specification of the nationality shares.
5. **Q19 — the financial centre's companies, by default.** By default, until the owner says otherwise: in Part A the centre's companies get no jobs and their count is reported; they are read, counted, name-checked for SYN-20 and given their identifiers in the canonical order, and are not written into the December snapshot — their resolved share by itself is under the suite's quality line. Open item: how a company of a register that publishes no activity stands in the feed (DAT-21, DAT-23), owner task S4-T01 or a later decision record.
6. **Q20 — `worksite_basis`.** The synthetic feed holds `unknown` only: no employment outside the private sector and no deployed worksite is generated (decision 0023 item 12; SYN-18), so no record carries the deployed flag.
7. **The calibration table's declared format.** The reader's minimum cell sizes and the salary level's rounding unit are properties of the table's declared format, read from a declared-format file by the reader and the median check — not manifest entries and not tuning constants. By default, until the owner says that the table's conditions may be stated in the repository, the real table's declared-format file stays outside the repository beside the table, and the committed stand-in carries its own declared-format file with invented values. The lead's reading of SYN-02, put to the owner: these values generate nothing; they are checks on an input (the reader refuses a table with a smaller cell, and the median check compares a rounded median with the table), so they belong to the input's declared format and not to the manifest or to the constants module of the generators. The owner's note and the advisor's reading supply the exact declared-format values; those values are not repeated in this record.
8. **The fields null in Part A.** `address_type` is null for every company (section 6.1: "null when no register file shows it"; no register file shows it). `registration_status`, `licence_status`, `licence_valid_from` and `licence_valid_to` are null in Part A as an interim state, not as a reading the contract allows: the register file shows the two statuses and the dates, and section 6.1 allows a null status only "when no register file shows it". Part A reads no status, expiry or cancellation field at all (the task's split), so its snapshot is not a finished `business_register.v1` month for these four fields. For the two dates section 6.1 gives a null its own meaning (`licence_valid_from`: not recorded; `licence_valid_to`: open-ended); in Part A's snapshot the null means neither, it means not read. No release is built from that snapshot, its README and `coverage.json` name the four fields and `establishment_nationality` as not read in Part A, and Part B fills them when a later decision record on the register's dates and statuses, drafted and not yet in the pack, has ruled their reading. `establishment_nationality` is null for every company in Part A, again as an interim state: the register file holds two nationality columns, so "null when the register shows none" (section 6.1) is not claimed; none of their values is a key of the owner's group list, neither column is read in Part A, and the reviewed mapping from what the register publishes to the field's closed list is an open item.
9. **The sandbox roles are a later pass.** The work on the new read-only mount roles of the `build-world` profile (the first part of Q11) leaves this pass and becomes its own pass after a later decision record of its own, with a security review, rules those roles; this record does not hold them. Until then the criterion "sandbox proof passes" of task S4-T02 is NOT PROVED for the new inputs; Part A proves the existing proofs unchanged, that every input reaches the code through an explicit argument or setting, and that the snapshot is staged in a scratch folder given as an argument.

## What does not change

Decisions 0022 and 0023 stand; the register files, the calibration table and the owner's nationality-group file stay outside the repository, read by the lead's own shell only (decision 0022 items 1, 2 and 8). No conformance guard, contract, threshold or tolerance changes; DAT-21 to DAT-24 stand. The December stock is final when Part A ends; the December snapshot is not — Part B may add the centre's rows and the fields that the later record on the register's dates and statuses rules on. No mount role, no PRIVATE input-reference kind and no manifest, sandbox or golden-list change is decided here.
