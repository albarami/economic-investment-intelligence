## Context

Decision 0023 item 12 restricts generation to confirmed future sources. Some method exclusions nevertheless treat generated inputs themselves as a reason not to build a method. The owner removes that reason: these sources stand for future real data, and the pipeline and methods must be ready for that substitution. This decision records design requirements, not a claim that the pipeline or a method has been implemented or validated on real data.

## Decision

1. **Confirmed sources only, equal method treatment.** Generate only data the owner knows can be obtained later; each generated source stands for that real source. Never exclude, narrow or discount a method because its input is generated today. Design, evaluate and include it as if the inputs were the real data represented. This does not authorise an additional source, invented company dates, ownership networks or missing source attributes.
2. **Real-data-ready.** Use one pipeline, contracts, code and tests for generated and real data, with a swap test per generated source on an invented real-shaped fixture. Match the real source's fields, volumes and keys. Models and parameters fitted on generated data are placeholders: the go-live gate re-fits and re-backtests them on real data. Intervals and error come from the method's own backtest or stated procedure. Pin versions, fix seeds and provide a deterministic CPU fallback. No worker or test reads real custodian records to prove this requirement.
3. **Methods enter scope.** Success scores, policy-lever simulation, survival and entry rates, and other methods previously excluded because inputs were generated, are included as tested-code methods. A success score is a criterion-based score, never a probability of venture success. A forecast shows its backtest interval, measured error, method and source; an estimate shows low, middle and high cases and drivers; a base rate shows counts and period; a ranking shows editable criteria and its rank-stability sentence. Rule 25's display requirements as amended by decision 0035 item 4 remain (no visible origin label; explicit backend origin and generator metadata under decision 0035 item 3). None becomes a verdict, probability or confidence claim, advice to act or a forecast of venture success. Missing fields and denominators still cause a refusal: unlinked Public licence counts do not become a survival cohort. A generated employment spell tests employment retention, not the survival of a firm. No named-business estimate is authorised.
4. **Aggregate realism.** Match sizes, activity mix, occupation mix, salary level and spread (not just medians), nationality-group shares, gender shares, age bands, hiring and leaving rates, seasonality, residence patterns, footfall by hour and day and their correlations as closely as possible, from the owner's aggregate calibration tables and cited public sources. Respect each attribute's permitted grain: age bands remain movement aggregates and worker gender follows decision 0037. The world build checks every calibrated ratio against its stated tolerance and reports pass or fail without any cell value. Tests use invented calibration fixtures of the same shape. Company size bands of the calibration tables and of the generated world are by recorded headcount: micro below 10 employees, small 10 to 49, medium 50 to 99 and large 100 and over (the owner's note of 2026-11-15); a public table keeps its own size classes under its own table code and is never re-banded to these. Calibration fit is not independent validation; existing exact checks and tolerances do not change.
5. **Not a copy.** Match aggregate shapes only. Reproduce no real person, private identifier, address or single-company calibration figure. Public register fields and joining keys retain decision 0022's separate authority; they are not private calibration targets. Every generated record carries its backend origin marker under [decision 0035](0035-what-the-audience-sees-names-no-data-origin-and-the-backend-marks-it.md) item 3, which fixes the origin field, its values `synthetic` and `simulated`, and the generator's name and version. A future authorised comparison with real data must show different records with the same shape.
6. **Rule 3 calibration exception.** The owner's aggregate calibration tables are read only by the world build on decision 0022's existing terms. They comprise the first table of decision 0022 and the further tables of the owner's note of 2026-11-15: a nationality-group mix; a gender mix by occupation group and activity; and a salary spread from the 10th to the 90th percentile, basic and net, by occupation group, activity and size band. No value is described here or put in a fixture, report, brief or log. Decision 0033's declared-format safeguards and unresolved sandbox roles remain; this decision does not invent a mount or reader setting.
7. **Unchanged controls.** Document 06, every privacy/security gate, attack size and tolerance, no owner networks, no model-written queries, deterministic tested calculation, and the ban on invented attributes of real places and businesses beyond decision 0022 remain. Rules 24 and 25's prohibited claims remain. Provenance and evidence status remain separate. Earlier decision records are retained as history; this record governs the passages it amends.
