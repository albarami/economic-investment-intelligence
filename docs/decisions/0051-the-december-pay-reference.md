## Context

Document 05 SYN-28 prescribes log-normal basic pay around occupation/activity cell means scaled to `wage.private_sector_mean` and `wage.restaurant_annual`. The S4-T03 Part A2 plan addendum, Q1–Q3, instead proposes declared salary quantiles and final repair of the original December net medians. Its design review's “DECISION RECORD NEEDED” identifies that distributional change as material under AGENTS.md rule 46. ## Decision items

1. **Exact net medians remain the level; compatible Public means lead distinct mean targets.** The reference is December 2025 monthly net pay before monthly draws and late filing (S4-T03 ruling 3; PLAN-A2 Q1). Decision 0022 item 2, the original salary-cell membership and the unchanged `rounded_median` equality remain exact. Replace ruling 4's “the calibration targets are the only level; no generator reads the Public means” with: exact private net medians remain the level, while compatible Public means lead their own mean targets at their supported grain, private means cross-checking them. Replace ruling 2's exclusion of SYN-28 Public anchors to that extent; its other scope and SYN-46 exclusion stand. S4-T11 must confirm basis, population, period and category compatibility under decision 0040 items 5 and 6. A `monthly_average_wage` is not automatically basic or net; annual compensation is not automatically December pay. Incompatible means remain a gap, without forced scaling or annual-to-monthly conversion; OI-03 remains. Scale provisional distributions at the admitted Public grain in fixed source/key order, before net-median repair, and remeasure every supported mean afterwards, including overlapping margins. Incompatible simultaneous targets stop world acceptance, never weaken the exact check (PLAN-A2 Q1–Q2).

2. **Spread groups remain separate from level cells.** Use decision 0036 item 6's basic and net salary spread, from the 10th to the 90th percentile, by occupation group, actual employer activity and employer December headcount band. Headcount is a count of employees from the complete December stock, not the covered subset: micro below 10 employees, small 10–49, medium 50–99, large 100 and over (decision 0036 item 4). Original occupation/activity/size-band salary-level cells, including pooled keys, remain intact; an original `s50_plus` level key does not distinguish medium from large. Join the separate memberships per job and check them separately. No Public size table is re-banded. Missing or ambiguous employer/class mappings refuse `PAY_CELL_MAPPING` (PLAN-A2 Q2; decision 0040 item 5).

3. **Declared-quantile construction replaces the log-normal December construction.** Require one unambiguous, non-suppressed series for each needed spread group and each basis, with declared quantiles and both endpoints. Absent or suppressed rows refuse `PAY_SPREAD_UNAVAILABLE`; duplicates, descending quantiles, missing endpoints or unsupported units refuse `PAY_SPREAD_INVALID`. There is no fallback to `wage.within_cell_sigma`, neighbouring cells or pooled percentiles. A later fallback needs an explicit ruling, labelled assumed coverage and pre-run fit criteria (PLAN-A2 Q2).

   Assign evenly spaced ranks within each spread group, permuted by its stream over canonically sorted jobs; a singleton uses the middle rank. Basic and net use shared ranks. Interpolate between declared quantiles in exact rational arithmetic, with flat tails at the endpoints. After item 1's mean scaling, sort net candidates within each original level cell, shift by target minus the exact central median, round to declared monetary precision with half-even ties, then pin the central value for an odd count or both central values for an even count to the target. Repair net medians last and require the unchanged `rounded_median` equality. Refuse non-positive or unrepresentable reference output as `PAY_REFERENCE_DOMAIN`. Refusal does not prove that no alternative construction can fit the targets. These are adopted construction assumptions, not source properties (PLAN-A2 Q2, A1–A2).

4. **Basic and net retain distinct units and constraints.** Both references are exact decimal money in QAR/month; quantile ranks, scaling ratios and sigma are dimensionless. Floor the basic companion at `wage.minimum` under SYN-27. Preserve a basic 10th percentile below that floor as an input conflict for S4-T12, never overwrite it; if the pinned criterion cannot pass, the world fails. Net's 10th percentile below the basic floor is not automatically invalid. No fixed allowance ratio substitutes for the basic spread series. Part B retains net = basic + extra_income − deductions and extra_income = allowances + overtime (ruling 6; document 03 section 6.3). Net minus basic is a signed difference, not automatically an allowance; a negative difference requires lawful deductions. Part A2 emits neither pay parts nor monthly wage rows (PLAN-A2 Q3).

5. **Coverage cannot silently change the exact population.** Apply an injected, reviewed policy for both activity and job eligibility. `covered_private` requires private sector and neither exclusion; excluded private jobs are `not_measured` and get no reference or wage record. Non-private stock rows refuse `non_private_sector`. Preserve ruling 5's no-keyword rule and `COVERAGE_POLICY_MISSING`; unresolved job eligibility refuses `COVERAGE_JOB_UNRESOLVED`. Do not infer casual, domestic or family status from occupation alone. Preserve the December rows and counts. If an exclusion removes a member needed by an original exact salary cell, refuse `PAY_CALIBRATION_POPULATION`, rather than recomputing its target over survivors (PLAN-A2 Q4, A3–A4; SYN-14).

6. **Fit acceptance belongs to S4-T12, not Part A2.** Pin estimator, population, period, ratio definitions and tolerance references before the fit run; missing declarations refuse `PAY_FIT_SPEC_MISSING`. Tail truncation, tiny cells, mean scaling, the basic floor and median repair can disturb spread or mean fit. S4-T12 measures the final candidates against the declared targets; exact medians are not a spread or mean pass. Under SYN-91 and decision 0036 items 4 and 6, private-fit reporting contains check identifier, tolerance reference and pass/fail, without target, observed value, residual or reconstructible cell label. No tolerance is invented here (PLAN-A2 Q1–Q3, Q5–Q6).

   The SYN-28 occupation-mean ordering clause is **kept**, with its unchanged `wage.mean_by_occupation` anchor and identical-order criterion, and **moved to S4-T12 for evaluation**, not imposed by overwriting December net medians. It is not re-anchored to private medians. F11 is **kept**, covered mean monthly wage over 2025 months against `wage.private_sector_mean`, within 10%, anchor year 2023. F12 is **kept**, covered occupation means against `wage.mean_by_occupation`, identical order. F13 is **kept**, restaurant ISIC 5610 mean annual pay against QAR 31,579 per year, within 15%, anchor year 2022 (`wage.restaurant_annual`). These existing targets and tolerances are not re-anchored or relaxed; their evaluation is **moved to S4-T12**, as ruling 4's Q18 clause and document 05 Q18 provide. Compatibility is checked under item 1: a gap is not a pass, and a December reference cannot stand in for annual or full-month-window evidence. S4-T12 must report unresolved incompatibility; this record grants no acceptance waiver (document 05 section 9, F11–F13; SYN-34 and SYN-35; decision 0040 Wiring and question classes).

7. **Preserve the December seam, privacy and the remaining task.** PLAN-A2 Q5–Q6 uses separate internal `december_coverage.csv`, `december_pay_reference.csv` and `pay_reference.json` artifacts through `read_december_stock`; it does not rebuild or write into the stock directory. Coverage keys equal stock keys; reference keys equal covered keys. Schemas, including nested metadata, are closed; hashes cover stock, declared formats, mappings/policy, calibration, manifest and selected Public receipts. Checks contain `check_id`, `tolerance_ref`, `passed`. The proposed `wages.a2.v1.rank` and `wages.a2.v1.tie` streams are scoped by canonical spread key without changing stock streams, identifiers or metadata; coverage uses no randomness. Publish staged artifacts after validation, with origin and generator name/version under decision 0035 item 3. No gender, nationality or age is copied into these artifacts; errors use fixed rule names with no private values or paths. Artifact/schema/stream choices remain A5, with the existing readers and seam authoritative.

   Decision 0022 item 2, `rounded_median`, all exact checks, privacy rules, security controls and tolerances are unchanged. S4-T02's December stock stays byte-identical on success and refusal. Part B retains monthly pay parts, ruling 6's net identity, housing/food minima, deduction reasons, heaping, employer effects, flat scales, drift and zero-pay/late-filing behaviour, and must revalidate final spread and identity (SYN-26–29; document 05 section 7.1). Decision 0050's homes are unchanged. No feed, linkage, residence, identifier-kind or employment-gender change is authorised by this record (PLAN-A2 Q3–Q8).

8. **Construction requires planted guards; no fit is claimed.** Apply PLAN-A2 Q7–Q8's tests before implementation, using invented same-shape fixtures and property-based tests for central values, exact half-even rounding, membership/count conservation, monetary precision, the floor and deterministic serialization. Each guard in items 1–7 needs a planted violation under AGENTS.md rule 37, including input reordering and unchanged stock bytes/hashes. Carry the amended D8 plants explicitly: non-positive shifted net refuses `PAY_REFERENCE_DOMAIN`; coverage that removes a required exact-cell member refuses `PAY_CALIBRATION_POPULATION`; a missing required key in each CSV fails exact key-set checks; unknown nested members in `checks`, `input_hashes`, `streams` and `output_hashes` fail schema closure. Plant private-value leaks and absent origin markers, as well as a median-passing candidate that fails spread and one that misses a Public mean; neither becomes a fit pass. Part B identity plants remain deferred, not passed. PLAN-A2 Q7–Q8's implementation boundary and lead mutation campaign remain separate implementation work.
