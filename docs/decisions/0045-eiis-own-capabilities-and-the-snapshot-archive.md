## Context

EII answers a company's decision at a particular place and time in Qatar: where to operate, whom to staff with, what demand and the supply chain look like, and what the economics need. The answer includes evidence that could change the decision, in Arabic and English, with every number traceable and every protected figure under the disclosure engine.

The owner's two notes make thirteen capabilities and the land and price family L1 to L7 EII's own acceptance list. The thresholds stated by that list are the existing 10, 20 and 30-minute staffing catchments and the 1,000 sensitivity runs. The notes also set the demonstration order, the privacy snap and own-data overlay as signature moments, and an archive for public tables whose history would otherwise be lost when a publisher replaces them.

The existing queue already covers most of the list. Reusing those tasks preserves their dependencies, privacy controls, method cards and reviews. The two new tasks are S2-T27 for the snapshot archive and S8-T18 for the own-data CSV overlay. Amendments add only missing acceptance detail to S2-T08, S2-T19, S6-T02, S6-T03, S6-T10, S6-T12, S6-T37, S7-T01, S7-T05, S7-T07, S7-T08, S7-T09, S7-T10, S8-T17 and S10-T03. L-697 supplies the archive's final reader, cadence, persistence and operational-file rulings.

This record changes documents and acceptance criteria, not code. It does not claim that EII, any capability, any task or any gate exists or passes.

## Decision

1. **The existing tasks carry the capabilities.** The mapping below is binding. A capability with existing coverage keeps that delivery path; an amendment names a genuine missing criterion. S2-T27 and S8-T18 are the complete set of new tasks. Each task keeps its real requirement IDs and dependencies, unchecked testable criteria, planted violations, named builder and reviewer personas, and queued status. S2-T27 is Deep because it pins the hashes of released inputs; S8-T18 is Deep because it creates a controlled company-data ingress exception.

2. **The golden scenario and its lead moments.** The Lusail conversation follows the extract's demonstration order: the Qatari-day journey; three sites and what would change your answer; staffing geography with the privacy snap; local demand linked to import origins; the bilingual evidence challenge with voice and conversation-test-lab evidence; then the own-data overlay and the 3D dusk and night-lights scrub. Capabilities 3, 1 and 5, and 2 supply the opening lead moments in that order. The price-and-access lens enters the Lusail conversation and the Explore inspector. The privacy snap and the own-data overlay with its "nothing sent" receipt are signature moments of the guided story and Explore. S6-T37, S8-T18 and S10-T03 own those integrations; the slice S8 and S10 gates name the moments without adding a threshold.

3. **The Qatari day and movement boundaries.** Presence remains split into workers, residents and visitors across weekday lunch, evening, Friday, Ramadan iftar, summer and the three event days. Map lighting and reachable access move with the selected hour. Visitor opportunity uses only decision 0023 item 16's one-way movement aggregates for nationality group, age band and gender, with counts and shares after PRV-134 treatment. Published hotel guests and nights by nationality and relative venue busyness remain separate Public components on their own geography and in the publisher's categories. No roaming-derived origin attribute or nationality proxy, crossed split, person-level row, or key joining movement to a worker, residence or company record is added. Public and simulated figures do not share a component.

4. **The public snapshot archive.** S2-T27 extends the existing fetch layer, registry, receipts and monthly register snapshots. Every fetched file is retained in the uncommitted local cache under a source, retrieval-date and SHA-256 path; an existing snapshot is never overwritten or removed. Equal bytes record a new check without a duplicate copy, while changed bytes create a new snapshot. A snapshot's retrieval time is immutable metadata; every successful check, whether or not it creates a snapshot, records its own check time in the index, and scheduling counts from the latest successful check, never from a snapshot's retrieval time alone. The index holds source, retrieval time, publisher version or modification date where stated, byte size and hash. Reads verify the hash. No fetched value without an open licence enters the repository, and automated tests use no network or key.

   The release-side readers that exist read an explicit reviewed snapshot reference by hash, with no latest-file fallback (lead ruling L-697 item 1): the frame (`frame/live.py` and every caller of its cached read), the selected-series source, the trade manifests, the review cache fixture (`review/cache_fixture.py`, both its cached read and its cached write), `review/activity.py`, `review/graph.py`, the data-test cache (`custodian/data_tests/cache.py`) and the spine triage reader (`scripts/spine_triage_io.py`). Each of these paths has its own older-of-two selection test and its own planted tamper refusal. S2-T08 applies the same rule to every public input of release assembly. S2-T19 gives every price a dated "as of" from the snapshot its hash names. S2-T27 does not own that price-producer criterion.

   There is no default cadence. Every registry row has an explicit closed cadence: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual, `as_published`, or `none` with a reason. The weekly sales bulletins are weekly. EII's recorded re-fetch schedule assigns the families (lead ruling L-700 item 3; it states when EII checks a source, never a claim about how often a publisher publishes): daily, due at 03:00 Asia/Qatar (00:00 UTC), for tender notices, event pages, incentive windows and investment-project pages; weekly for registers, GIS layers and supplier credentials; monthly for register snapshots and for each Overture release, because the advisor's reading of 2026-11-16 reports that the publisher removes a release after at most 60 days (S2-T27 records that statement in document 04's Overture rows with its public page and retrieval date, or states it as unconfirmed), so a release not captured within its window would be lost to the archive; and on release (`as_published`) for statistical series, with every revision kept as its own snapshot. A failed retrieval never establishes that a source has closed or stopped. Precedence: only EII's fixed daily, weekly and monthly family schedules state when EII checks regardless of the publisher, so a row of one of those families keeps its family's cadence when the publisher's frequency is unknown (a fixture proves it). The statistical-series family depends on the publisher: a statistical series takes `as_published` only where document 04 records a publisher-stated publication-driven schedule, and a statistical series without one takes `none` with `frequency_unconfirmed` and no family. Any row with no fixed-schedule family and no recorded publisher frequency becomes `none` with `frequency_unconfirmed`, never `as_published` or an invented period (a planted unconfirmed row, including an unconfirmed statistical series, that acquires either is refused). The offline due listing uses UTC calendar anniversaries with end-of-month clamping for monthly, quarterly and annual cadences, elapsed UTC days for daily cadence, elapsed weeks for weekly cadence, each counted from the latest successful check, so an equal-byte re-fetch resets due without creating or altering a snapshot (a fixture proves it). `as_published` produces `publication_check_required`; a source never fetched is due; `none` is excluded with its reason.

   The archive remains immutable in the uncommitted local cache. Off-machine survival is proposed open item OI-[LEAD-1]: the owner manages a separately held copy and the lead runs a restore-and-hash check. No tool-built remote integration, remote path, key or network dependency is added. Comtrade `state.json` and `state.lock` remain mutable operational files outside the archive; `coverage.json` is an archived fetch output. Each classification has a test.

5. **Land and price is a headline family.** Card C41 separates bare-land deals from built property and shows guide price against recorded deal price by district, use and quarter, with deal counts and a dated "as of". S6-T14 supplies the backtest-bounded forecast or short-history refusal. Affordability is years of pay for a Public unit price against a published wage benchmark, never a ratio to a protected pay figure. The price-and-access view puts price per square foot beside deterministic distance to the nearest planned station and its protection zone, always planned and never operating.

   The cheaper-location view places dated land-cost context and the user's own rent beside talent reach, hourly presence and gateway travel time, without a net-benefit score or recommendation. The readiness screen uses tested, visible criteria: permitted zoning and an empty-looking footprint, powered and cooled zone context, peak-hour reach, and a recorded bare-land price below the district guide. It never calls a plot available, approved, vacant or for sale, and utility context never becomes a connection or capacity claim. The district commercial guide price is land-cost context in break-even; the user's own rent is the sole rent, and no purchase price becomes a monthly financing cost without explicit user financing inputs. Property-price trend and bank credit by activity stay separate dated series; credit is not a mortgage rate, approval or financing offer.

6. **The own-data exception is narrow.** S8-T18 expressly amends the no-browser-file clauses of ARC-128, DAT-149 and DAT-150, BE-109 and BE-118, FE-120 and their scans for this overlay alone. The existing closed-key sample-file route stays unchanged. The new local same-origin route accepts only the existing closed branch and delivery CSV schemas of S8-T08, in API-process memory bound to the current session and seat. It is disabled in remote mode. Other pages and routes retain the file-body prohibition.

   No CSV row, coordinate, derived fact or overlay brief enters a release, persistent store, browser storage, cache, log beyond call and status, memo, egress record, backup or freeze bundle. None reaches a model. Reset, seat change, session end and restart delete it; another seat or session cannot read it. Deterministic read functions and `eii_core.calc` perform every calculation. The ephemeral receipt says precisely that no firm row left the local API process and no firm row or derived value was sent to a model or an external destination; authorised derived facts may reach the local browser. Exact-payload egress checks and post-session persistence scans back that statement; an uninspectable path cannot produce a passing receipt. The receipt is neither a signed file nor a formal privacy guarantee.

7. **Every extract limit is a refusal or a mandatory limitation.** The implementing cards and catalogue entries carry the following rules, with planted violations in their named tasks:
   - Device panels are not unique visitors. Presence is not purchases. Calibration fit is not independent validation. No unique-visitor, purchase or independent-validation claim is allowed from those inputs.
   - Wages are not spending power. Registrations are not operating outlets. A licensed-versus-operating difference is computed only over a reviewed common activity, geography and period; otherwise it is `NO_DATA`, and neither source is relabelled.
   - Electricity 2026 covers January to August only. No later-month electricity claim is allowed.
   - No open rent, sales, vacancy or live-events-calendar source is added or inferred. A sale price is not a rent. A guide price is not a market or deal price. Planned rail is not operating rail. No plot availability or approval is inferred from zoning, an empty footprint, utilities, access or price.
   - Public school-location and capacity tables are not confirmed. `students_muni` stays municipal context and never becomes school enrolment, capacity or an invented school catchment; unsupported school figures return `NO_DATA`.
   - Hotel and visitor publications retain their own categories and geography and never join synthetic records or combine with protected or simulated figures. Public tender matching never asserts a bidder, winner, supplier relationship, contract or operating outlet, and uncertain bilingual matches remain selectable candidates.
   - The personal-data law of 2016, subscriber-location analytics rules, permissions for special-nature processing and cross-border use of real aggregates remain legal review boundaries for any real-data deployment. This decision builds no roaming or device-feed integration and adds no legal clearance claim.

8. **Decision 0035 stands in full.** No audience surface names data origin through a visible class, word, texture, label, badge, watermark, accessible name, export, filename, URL or speech, and no persistent illustrative-scenario notice is added. Backend origin fields and generator name and version remain explicit. Method honesty, source chains, suppression reasons, intervals, backtest errors, rank stability, visible scenario assumptions and limitation sentences remain visible. No generated figure is called official, measured, observed from a real register or supplied by an outside publisher it does not have. No comparison or superlative claim is added.

9. **Controls and scope do not otherwise change.** Rules 10 and 16 to 25 remain intact. Models never calculate, write queries or prose, or receive company rows. Closed catalogues, application-owned templates, Fact chips, sanad lineage, release-only boundaries, privacy parameter sets, attack sizes, tolerances, sandbox and store parity, and the accounting gate are unchanged. No existing gate, refusal or security check is weakened. The new CSV route adds no data source to the release and no model tool. Further substantive changes need another decision record under rule 46.

- **Build queue.** S2-T27 and S8-T18 join the queue as Deep. S2-T19 and S2-T08 divide price dating from release-side snapshot reads. The amendments named in Context make the thirteen capabilities and L1 to L7 testable without duplicate tasks. S6-T20, S6-T21, S6-T09 and other unchanged tasks retain their existing criteria rather than receiving restatements.
- **Gates.** The slice S2 gate requires reviewed-hash release reads, dated prices and truthful unconfirmed-frequency handling. The slice S8 and S10 gates name the golden-scenario lead moments and own-data receipt. Existing thresholds, attack suites and review requirements remain unchanged; S6 and S7 retain their existing gates because this record adds no separate end-to-end golden-scenario run there.
- **Story and Explore.** S10-T03 carries the demonstration order and S6-T37 carries the privacy snap and land-and-price inspector. S8-T18 supplies the shared overlay used by both. A district or parcel fact shown for a building selection retains its actual Public geography and never becomes an invented building attribute.
- **Archive operation.** The local archive accumulates hash-verified history while the offline due listing makes re-fetch state inspectable. Unconfirmed publisher frequency is visible rather than guessed. Off-machine survival remains open under OI-[LEAD-1], and unconfirmed frequencies remain open under OI-[LEAD-2].
- **Documentation.** Document 14 section 6 maps every capability and land/price item to its real delivery path and its slice sections name the new tasks. docs/README.md lists 0045 among accepted records in its accepted-record sentence and Decision records table. TASKS.md carries all criteria, dependencies, requirements, personas and gate sentences.
- **No implementation claim.** These are future acceptance criteria. No archive, overlay, card, task completion, gate pass, legal approval or real-data readiness is asserted.
