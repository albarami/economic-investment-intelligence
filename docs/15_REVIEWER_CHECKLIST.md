## In brief

- Every task ends with an independent review by a reviewer from a different maker than the writer.
- The reviewer reviews exactly one commit, applies every check in sections 2 and 3, and returns APPROVED or REJECTED in the fixed format of section 4.
- The reviewer changes no file. A REJECTED verdict always gives, for each finding, the file, the line, what is wrong and the exact fix.
- APPROVED means no open finding. There is no "approved with comments".
- Round 1 is the full review. A later round still answers every check but may reject only for the kinds of finding in REV-39; a check whose only defects are other points reads OPEN, and those points are proposed open items, closed before the slice gate (REV-41).
- One designated reviewer of a maker other than the builder's gives the verdict. Extra reviewers are advisory (REV-43).
- Security is strict in two places only: nothing secret, real or private in the repository (REV-14, REV-25, REV-28), and numbers and claims right (REV-15, REV-20 to REV-24, REV-26, REV-29). Hardening beyond an acceptance criterion in [TASKS.md](../TASKS.md) is never a verdict finding in any round; it goes to [17_DEPLOYMENT_HARDENING.md](17_DEPLOYMENT_HARDENING.md) with its origin ([decision 0018](decisions/0018-priorities-and-amendments-to-decision-0015.md)).
- House-style points, the 500-line target per code file included, are targets: a reviewer may note them, and they are never a verdict finding (REV-16, [decision 0018](decisions/0018-priorities-and-amendments-to-decision-0015.md) item 14).
- Evidence counts; assurances do not. A check passes only when the reviewer has seen its evidence.

## 1. Rules of the review

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| REV-01 | The reviewer must come from a different maker than the writer of the commit. The verdict names the reviewer by maker and model only, never by a personal name. |
| REV-02 | The review covers exactly one commit, named by its full 40-character hash, and that commit must be the head of the task's pull request. A verdict on any other commit is void. |
| REV-03 | The reviewer changes no file: no edit, no commit, no push, no formatting fix, no baseline update. The reviewer may run commands that leave tracked files unchanged, such as `./eii test` and `python3 scripts/check_docs.py`. |
| REV-04 | The reviewer reads, before judging: the task and its acceptance criteria in [TASKS.md](../TASKS.md), the full diff against the base branch, the evidence entries in [PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md) for this commit, and the documents the task touches. |
| REV-05 | Every check in sections 2 and 3 receives PASS, FAIL, OPEN or N/A with its evidence. N/A needs one sentence saying why the check does not apply to this commit. OPEN marks a check whose only defects are points outside the kinds of finding that may reject (REV-39); it names the ids of its proposed open items and does not reject. |
| REV-06 | One FAIL makes the verdict REJECTED. APPROVED requires every check to be PASS, OPEN or a justified N/A, and no finding; a commit also counts as APPROVED under REV-40. After round 1, a check is FAIL only for a finding of the kinds in REV-39. A design-audit Minor, as defined in [11_DESIGN_DIRECTION.md](11_DESIGN_DIRECTION.md) section 16, is recorded in the design audit with an owner and a target slice and is not a verdict finding. A design-audit Blocker or Major is a FAIL of that slice gate and a verdict finding. |
| REV-07 | Each finding must cite the check or the requirement it breaks. A preference is not a finding. A reviewer who thinks a settled decision is wrong raises it with the lead as a proposed open item, not as a finding. |
| REV-08 | After a REJECTED verdict, the writer fixes, the lead re-runs the evidence, and the reviewer reviews the new commit. Round 1 is the full review; in every later round the reviewer still answers every check, within the limits of REV-39. |
| REV-09 | The verdict is posted on the pull request, and the lead copies it unchanged into [PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md). |
| REV-39 | After round 1 a review still answers every check, but a verdict finding may only be: (a) an earlier finding that is not fixed; (b) a defect in, or caused by, lines that the task's own patch changed since the commit last reviewed (changes that come only from updating the base do not count); (c) a violation of REV-14, REV-15, REV-20 to REV-29 or REV-38 anywhere in the pull request; (d) an acceptance criterion of the task that is not met. From the second confirmation review on, kind (b) no longer rejects. Every other valid point goes under PROPOSED OPEN ITEMS, outside the verdict. The brief for round 1 says so, so that everything the reviewer can find is reported in round 1. If a reviewer puts a point outside these kinds into the verdict, the lead asks an advisor once, and the advisor's ruling on the kind of the point stands. A point the advisor rules out of the verdict leaves it for good and is never an earlier finding later; a verdict left with no finding counts as APPROVED for that commit. Hardening beyond an acceptance criterion of the task in [TASKS.md](../TASKS.md) is never a verdict finding, in any round: it goes to [17_DEPLOYMENT_HARDENING.md](17_DEPLOYMENT_HARDENING.md) with its origin and is not built now. Security stays strict in two places only: nothing secret, real or private reaches the repository (REV-14, REV-25, REV-28), and numbers and claims stay right (REV-15, REV-20 to REV-24, REV-26, REV-29); every other security requirement is built in its simplest form that meets its acceptance criterion ([decision 0018](decisions/0018-priorities-and-amendments-to-decision-0015.md)). The same limits apply when a plan is reviewed again. |
| REV-40 | Every verdict finding is fixed before the merge. If round 3 is REJECTED and the lead judges its findings valid, the findings are fixed by a stronger builder from a maker other than the reviewer's, and the same reviewer confirms under REV-39. There is no limit on confirmation reviews: the same reviewer keeps confirming until APPROVED. There is no blocked end: if the same finding is NOT FIXED twice in a row, the lead fixes it, the advisor rules on that finding, and the advisor checks the lines of the lead's fix for kinds (c) and (d) of REV-39; if the advisor rules that it is not fixed, the lead applies the fix the advisor names and asks again. When every finding of the last verdict is fixed by the reviewer's word or the advisor's ruling, or a verdict is left with no finding, the commit counts as APPROVED. Before every merge the lead reads the proposed open items of the last verdict, and fixes before the merge any one that belongs to a kind that may reject. CI that stays red is handled the same way: the lead finds the cause and never weakens a check ([decision 0018](decisions/0018-priorities-and-amendments-to-decision-0015.md)) |
| REV-41 | Each proposed open item names the requirement it concerns and its evidence. The proposed items of an approved pull request are entered in [OPEN_ITEMS.md](../OPEN_ITEMS.md) by the next pull request's ledger commit, never on the approved head. An item from a plan review goes into that task's build brief. An item raised by the review of a slice's follow-up task, or by a gate re-review, goes to the next slice's follow-up task; in the last slice, into the final report. Each slice ends with a follow-up task before its gate, listed in [TASKS.md](../TASKS.md), with its own branch, its own pull request and a full independent review. It closes each item by a fix, or by one line saying why the item is not valid or is out of scope under REV-42; its reviewer checks every closure. The gate is not passed while an item of the slice is open. The findings of a rejected gate review are fixed in a gate-fix task with its own entry in [TASKS.md](../TASKS.md) ([decision 0018](decisions/0018-priorities-and-amendments-to-decision-0015.md)) |
| REV-42 | The repository's own checks (the tree check, the documentation check, the hooks, the commit guard and the CI scripts) protect against mistakes in three settings: a fresh clone, the CI runner, and a checkout used through the documented commands, including ordinary differences between machines such as personal git configuration, locale or a linked path. Outside their scope is only: someone on the same machine replacing the checker, git or a hook program; an index flag set by hand; a race by a second local process. Anything that happens without anyone meaning it (a staged file that differs from the work tree, a busy port, a left-over process, another tool version) is a mistake, not tampering, and is in scope ([decision 0018](decisions/0018-priorities-and-amendments-to-decision-0015.md)). This never covers an input whose pin, digest or checksum must be verified: dependencies, downloads, images, bundles, releases. Line-ending conversion and personal git configuration are in scope. For the generator sandbox the launcher must build and verify the documented mounts and namespaces, and the guarantee covers what the launched program can read, write and reach. A point that needs such tampering is recorded as out of scope in one line and needs no fix, unless it is already a verdict finding. |
| REV-43 | One designated reviewer of a maker other than the builder's gives the verdict. Any extra reviewer is advisory: the lead judges each of that reviewer's points. A valid point of a kind that may reject in that round (REV-39 in a later round, any check in round 1) is fixed in the same round; every other valid point becomes a proposed open item (REV-41). Only the designated reviewer's verdict decides ([decision 0018](decisions/0018-priorities-and-amendments-to-decision-0015.md)) |

### 1.1 Order of work

1. Confirm the commit hash is the head of the pull request and that CI on it is green.
2. Read the task, its acceptance criteria and its scope in [TASKS.md](../TASKS.md).
3. Read the full diff. Read the diff of tests, CI configuration, lint configuration and thresholds first and line by line, because that is where checks get weakened.
4. Read the evidence entries for this commit in [PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md) and compare each recorded command, exit code and hash with what CI shows.
5. Re-run `./eii test` and `python3 scripts/check_docs.py` where the machine allows it, and record the exit codes in the verdict.
6. Apply every check in sections 2 and 3, then write the verdict in the format of section 4.

Each card, list and map legend carries collapsed credits naming the agency as publisher, the data gatherer as the page names it, the date read and a link to the dashboard, with the page's caveat: the data "includes estimates of publicly announced projects" and "does not capture all investment projects". Capital and jobs are labelled as announced estimates, never added to measured figures or shown beside them without that label, and a project is never joined to synthetic data, the attached records of a register company included. Named synthetic firms are retired ([decision 0022](decisions/0022-real-register-companies-with-a-synthetic-world.md)). A register company carries the fields the register publishes for it, person names excluded, and the employee count of its attached records; its wages, wage bill and other labour and residence details never appear on its card, in a list or on any other screen except as released aggregates, and it gets no Simulated figure, estimate, share or score; a list of register companies is ordered only by tested code over criteria the user can see and change, never by a company's wages or another unreleased value. No Simulated or Synthetic figure is presented as a named building's or business's footfall. The site estimate and the catchment-share scenario name no competitor: the user's outlet and the competitor cells by category and distance band are the only split, with no cell or field naming a business, and a named competitor keeps only its Public facts and its distance (a register company also its card's employee count). |
| REV-38 | Typed-decision gateway absent from the serving plane | The typed-decision gateway is absent from the serving plane and from run time. The only allowed screen text is the Proof room lineage line, "N activity codes; review order machine-assisted; every mapping signed by a person." A score, a badge, a panel or a live call fails the check. The import-boundary test shows that `eii_product`, `core`, `custodian` and `web` do not import `eii_gateway`, and no run-time code reads `EII_DECISION_PROVIDER` or `JEV_PRO_API_KEY`. |

## 4. Verdict format

The verdict is plain text in exactly this shape. Every check from REV-10 to REV-29 and REV-38 appears once, in order.

```text
VERDICT: APPROVED | REJECTED
TASK: <task ID from TASKS.md>
COMMIT: <full 40-character hash reviewed>
REVIEWER: <maker and model of the reviewer>
CHECKS:
  REV-10 PASS <evidence>
  REV-11 PASS <evidence: command, exit code, run by>
  REV-12 OPEN P1 <evidence>
  ...
  REV-29 N/A <why it does not apply>
  REV-38 PASS <evidence>
FINDINGS:
  F1 (REV-nn; after round 1 also its kind: a, b, c or d of REV-39)
    File: <repository-relative path>
    Line: <line number or range>
    Wrong: <what is wrong, with the requirement it breaks>
    Fix: <the exact change, and the test or command that will prove it>
PROPOSED OPEN ITEMS:
  <none, or one item per valid point outside the verdict: P1 (REV-nn) <requirement concerned> <evidence>>
```

In a round after the first, the block `EARLIER FINDINGS:` stands between `REVIEWER:` and `CHECKS:`, with one line per earlier finding: its number, `FIXED` or `NOT FIXED`, and the evidence.

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| REV-30 | A REJECTED verdict must list at least one finding, and every finding must give the file, the line, what is wrong and the exact fix. "Improve", "clean up" or "consider" is not a fix. |
| REV-31 | An APPROVED verdict must read `FINDINGS: none`. |
| REV-32 | The fix must be specific enough that the writer can apply it without asking: name the change, and name the test or command whose result will show it is done. |

An illustrative finding (the path and line are examples, not files of this repository):

```text
  F1 (REV-21)
    File: web/src/cards/ResidentsCard.tsx
    Line: 57
    Wrong: the housing-split share is rendered as a raw string, outside the
           Fact chip, so it has no provenance mark (breaks REV-21).
    Fix: render the share through the Fact chip with its fact identifier,
         remove the raw string, and show the numeral lint and the
         provenance audit passing on this card.
```

## Open questions and assumptions

- ASSUMPTION: the verdict is posted on the pull request and copied into PROGRESS.md by the lead (REV-09), because the owner's method needs one durable record per task and the pull request is where the commit lives.
- There is no "approved with comments" (REV-06, REV-40): every verdict finding is fixed before the merge. A valid point that a later round may not reject for (REV-39), or that needs tampering outside the scope of REV-42, is not a comment on an approval: it is a proposed open item, closed by the slice's follow-up task before the gate, or recorded there as out of scope in one line (REV-41). The owner decided this on 2026-11-12 ([decision 0015](decisions/0015-review-rounds-and-the-scope-of-repository-checks.md), amended by [decision 0018](decisions/0018-priorities-and-amendments-to-decision-0015.md)); it replaces the earlier assumption that every round is a full review.
- Decision 0018 leaves no new assumption. Hardening that earlier reviews raised beyond an acceptance criterion (tooling, sandbox, hooks, commit guard, CI scripts) is a register entry in [17_DEPLOYMENT_HARDENING.md](17_DEPLOYMENT_HARDENING.md), not a finding. REV-18 stays a general check in sections 2 and 4 and is no longer a kind (c) safety check after round 1.
- Open items touching this document: OI-01 (main model key supplied for go-live and unused during the build; template writer and recorded provider used throughout the build), OI-09 (native Arabic sign-off is separate from this review), OI-13 (GitHub secret scanning and push protection), OI-14 (no run-time use of the typed-decision gateway before an approved route), OI-35 (which parameter set is exported; default A until the owner or a data owner decides, needed by the slice S1 gate), OI-36 (layout signals outside the acceptance model; moved to [17_DEPLOYMENT_HARDENING.md](17_DEPLOYMENT_HARDENING.md) as a real-deployment item, while version 1 still states the limit) and OI-37 (independent review before slice S5).
