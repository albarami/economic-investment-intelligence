## Data, public-repository and boundary rules

3. Use synthetic custodian-type labour and residence records only. Every person is invented; the companies are the real ones of the public register, and their labour and residence records are synthetic; no real custodian record, field, file, count or parameter is ever read, except the owner's aggregate calibration tables, read only by the world build on the terms of decision 0022: its first table and the further tables of the owner's note of 2026-11-15, a nationality-group mix, a gender mix by occupation group and activity, and a salary spread from the 10th to the 90th percentile, basic and net, by occupation group, activity and size band (decision 0036); no value of those tables is described; and except the company-level columns of the owner's company workbook named in [decision 0074](docs/decisions/0074-company-level-fields-of-the-owners-company-workbook.md) item 1, read only by the world build and the lead's own shell for counting on the terms of that record and of decision 0033 item 1, and except the company activity, activity group and sector columns named in that record's item 6, read only by the lead's own shell for counting on the terms of that item, with no value of those columns described. Synthetic records are built only for the confirmed sources of decision 0023 item 12 (the employment records of private-sector individuals with their occupations and nationality group, the wages and the residence addresses, all attached to the register companies, and movement at the grain the pack plans: presence and movement by area and hour, with origin and destination); every other input is public data or a company's own input, and no synthetic data stands in for any other source.
4. Simulate mobility, footfall and geofencing locally. Never contact a telecom operator or use a real mobility feed; a published relative-busyness figure of a named venue is a Public fact, not such a feed. Simulate road traffic locally by default; the optional live traffic provider of decision 0019 is off by default, shows its flow tiles and traffic-aware drive-time areas with the provider's attribution, stores nothing of a response, and is never needed by a test, a gate, a command or the guided story.
5. Cite every Public input with publisher, dataset or page, licence and retrieval date; preserve its hash in the source registry.
6. Keep provenance (Public, Synthetic, Simulated, Suppressed) separate from evidence status (Observed, Inferred, Still to test). "Not measured" is a coverage label, not an evidence status.
7. Give every number provenance and lineage. A fact without a complete source chain is not a fact: fail its build, chip and claim validation.
8. Never invent an attribute for a real named place or business, except the synthetic labour and residence records attached to a register company (decision 0022). An `area` subject accepts Public, Synthetic, Simulated and Inferred slots; `place_public` and `fdi_project` accept Public slots only; a `register_company`, a real company of the public register, accepts Public slots and the employee count of its attached records, and its other labour and residence facts appear only in analyses released under document 06 and in its own seat (decision 0023 item 15). Named synthetic firms are retired. No other subject accepts a Synthetic or Simulated slot.
9. Use visibly synthetic identifiers that never imitate real registrations, establishments or personal identifiers. Fail on real-name collisions and on bare digit runs of exactly eight or eleven digits.
10. Under decision 0037, the generated employment records carry `gender` in the real-record format, drawn only from the owner’s gender mix by occupation group and activity; without that table the field is null and release coverage says "not drawn". Only document 06 may release gender statistics, under the same cell rules, differencing and attack tests as nationality group. Gender is never displayed for a person, never a joining key, and never copied onto residence or company records; no other sex or gender proxy is added. Public sex splits never supply this draw or join these records; only the existing movement-anchor exception remains. Movement retains its separate exception (decision 0023 item 16): movement aggregates carry age band, gender and nationality group as one-way splits of the visitors of an area and hour, and of an origin-destination pair, as counts and shares; never a person-level row, never a key that joins to a worker, residence or company record, and every cell under the minimum-count rule of document 06. Nationality enters in three forms only: the synthetic worker records carry the nationality group in the format of the real records, and the register's establishment nationality is a company attribute (decision 0022); movement aggregates carry the same nationality groups and one more group, international visitors (decision 0023 item 16). Each may be used as a dimension of released statistics under document 06, and no other nationality attribute, dimension or proxy is added. Public figures split by sex or nationality appear only as Public facts with source and year, in the source's own categories, on their own geography, and are never joined to a synthetic record, used to segment worker, residence or company records, or combined with a protected figure; the movement generator may use them, summed over the source's own categories, as cited anchors of its splits, and the world build may use a public nationality figure, summed over the source's own categories to the nationality groups the synthetic worker records carry, solely as a cited calibration anchor of their nationality draw with source and year, the public figure leading and private calibration a cross-check, never joined to a record, apportioned to a drawn area or combined with a protected figure and adding no attribute (decision 0040 item 6). A tabulated figure stays on the source's own geography and is never apportioned to a drawn area; whole cells of a published population grid may be summed over an area.
11. The product must never import custodian code or reach `data/vault/`. Only a verified release crosses from the custodian plane to the product plane.
12. Do not weaken privacy invariants, security controls, their tests, attack sizes or tolerances. No fallback is allowed for sandbox enforcement, store parity or any security check. Evaluate privacy parameter sets A and B only inside the custodian plane and export exactly one selected set's protected tables. Never tune a privacy rule to make an area look finer. A window is never exported under two treatments (PRV-120). `window_key` is the SHA-256 of canonical JSON holding the protected `period` and the four name-ordered `{name, sha256}` pairs for `business_register.v1`, `employment.v1`, `residence.v1` and `wages.v1`, using each feed's manifest `feed_sha256`; nothing else enters it. Continuous integration checks the export register alone: existing lines stay unchanged, with one `protected_sha256` per `window_key` and one `window_key` per `world_seed`. A freeze is refused on either uniqueness conflict; after a freeze, a change of rules version, exported set, treatment code or world generator requires a new `world_seed` and a new build before another freeze. `./eii freeze` is run twice with no new flag: the first run writes the bundle, checksum list and pending `freeze.json` and appends the register line; after normal merge, the second run from the containing base-branch commit builds and verifies the application archive, writes its checksum and marks `freeze.json` complete. For a frozen release only, install and start refuse with exit 7 unless the installed application's register holds the line and `freeze.json` says complete; the remedy is to finish the freeze, not change the seed. No signed receipt file is added.
13. Keep `data/vault/`, every folder that git ignores or does not track, and every path outside the repository off-limits. Tests may use only their documented repository, cache and temporary paths. The sole imagery exception of [decision 0079](docs/decisions/0079-dated-gis-snapshot-and-structured-address-search.md) item 3 lets the lead-shell dedicated snapshot mode read/write its configured external D: sibling root, and explicitly selected release assembly read only its verified named immutable files through read-only sandbox bindings; no ordinary job or product reads that root, no test reads D:, and no other path is admitted.
14. Put no secret in a file, log, ledger, brief, prompt or error. Read secrets only from the environment; never commit `.env`.
15. Write no private path, personal name, e-mail address, confidential plan, realistic identifier or named existing product comparison. Public data attribution is required and is not a comparison.

## Accuracy, models and screen rules

16. No model ever calculates. Every displayed number comes from deterministic, tested code in `eii_core.calc`, the release build, or a fixed application-schema read function listed by the data contracts and covered by tests. Nothing else computes, rounds or compares a number.
17. Comparisons, rounding, ranking and selection of numbers are calculations too. Models never perform them; code returns comparison facts for catalogue slots.
18. No model writes SQL, Cypher, another query, a tool argument outside its closed schema, or a number. Numeric arguments come only from closed schema values, typed user inputs or returned facts.
19. Apply the sanad rule: every fact resolves through fact → release table → method and version → source, licence, retrieval date and hash.
20. Never weaken the accounting gate. Check sources, assumptions, invention, scope and limits before display; fall back to the template writer and log failure.
21. No model-written prose reaches the screen. Models select and order catalogue claims, components, fragments, questions and stage directives; application-owned templates render every word.
22. Keep claim, component, fragment and question catalogues closed, versioned and typed. Reject unknown keys, properties, wrong fact slots and invalid layouts; one repair, then template fallback.
23. Render every numeral only through the Fact chip family. Build-time scans cover templates; do not substitute a run-time scan of model prose for structural prevention. One named exception, `verbatim-quotation` ([decision 0029](docs/decisions/0029-the-agencys-pages-as-verbatim-quotations.md)): a numeral that is a character of the stored `pub_investqa_page.page_text` may render, unparsed, as one opaque run inside the body element of the `source_quotation` component only; that text is never a fact, a claim, a question slot, a stage directive or a model input, and every other numeral, the quotation's caption included, stays in the Fact chip family. A quotation under this exception is not a forecast, an estimate or advice of this product.
24. Never claim real custodian data, a Synthetic or Simulated fact about a real place, a Simulated or Synthetic footfall figure, or an estimate, share or score, attributed to a named building or business, certified or formal privacy, inferiority of an existing public service, ministry endorsement or operation, a forecast of the success of a venture, a forecast, estimate or base rate that tested code did not compute, or lookup of a firm, owner or individual. Showing a register company's public card and its employee count, under rule 8, is not a lookup (decision 0022); looking up an owner or an individual stays forbidden, and so do the other prohibited claims. Read with decision 0035 item 4: no generated figure is described as official, measured, observed from a real register or coming from a ministry; its visible source chain names its actual method and producing pipeline with version, never an outside publisher it does not have.
25. Give no go/no-go verdict, probability or confidence claim, and no advice to act. A ranking, fit or suggestion is shown only as the output of tested code over criteria the user can see and change, with its rank-stability sentence. A forecast is shown only with its interval from the backtest, its measured backtest error, its method and its source; an estimate with its low, middle and high cases, its main drivers (decision 0035 removes the visible origin label and retains explicit backend origin and generator metadata); a base rate with its counts and period; a user's scenario with every assumption visible and editable, never called a forecast. Show evidence, limitations and the cheapest next test.

## Offline typed-decision gateway

26. Keep `gateway/` (`eii_gateway`) behind the stage only, imported only by tooling under `evals/` and `scripts/`; nothing under `core/`, `custodian/` or `product/` imports it or reads its settings.
27. Default `EII_DECISION_PROVIDER` to `none`. Every gate, the guided story, `./eii up`, preflight and freeze must work with provider `none` and no network.
28. A gateway answer is decision evidence only: never a fact, number, privacy decision, gate, refusal, tool argument, recommendation, Arabic approval or authority.
29. Never send visitor-typed text, real custodian data, real identifiers, credentials, real company questions or text from another project.
30. Use only the five closed classes: `spine_match`, `router_miss_finder`, `brief_second_reader`, `question_route` (shadow, team questions only) and `string_parity` (experiment first).
31. Gateway results may only order human review, flag a report, or become a deterministic rule/test after human confirmation. `brief_second_reader` is advisory and raise-only.
32. Store offline decision records under `evals/typed_decisions/runs/`. There is no `app.decision_record` table and no run-time gateway call in version 1.

## Workflow and evidence

33. Use one task per branch and pull request, with Conventional Commits. Change only the task's scope and update affected documents in the same pull request. Cite a pack document by a stable anchor (a requirement ID, a section heading, a task ID, a rule number or the numbered item of a decision record), never by line number. Exception: under [decision 0032](docs/decisions/0032-approved-tasks-of-one-slice-may-merge-as-a-train.md), approved tasks of one slice may use one train branch and pull request. Each task is approved on its own local commit by another maker; its dependencies are merged or in the same train. One ledger holds every task's own rows and verdict. The six required hosted checks run once on the joined head; the lead runs the whole test with a fresh seed on the exact merge result; head checks are unchanged. One confirmation by a maker other than the one that joined the train confirms that the joined head holds exactly the approved commits and the records. The lead proves unchanged task changes by a script, file by file, listing each difference forced by the join and its reason; a forced code or test difference needs that task's own confirmation by another maker or the task leaves the train. The records are ledger rows, TASKS.md status lines, open items and pin headings added by the ledger. A joined head that fails a check is split and the parts retried. Every task keeps its own plan, build, checks and review; gates are unchanged. Decision records and other documents-only changes keep their own pull requests unless they are a task's own documents. Under [decision 0039](docs/decisions/0039-the-next-train-may-be-checked-while-the-previous-one-merges.md), the next pull request may open and run hosted checks, train confirmation and the lead's ledger while the previous hosted run proceeds; after that merge, bring its head onto the new main: a records-only move keeps approval with its diff attached, while a code or test move needs a new confirmation. Rerun required hosted checks on the moved head and, in parallel, run the lead's whole test with a fresh seed on the exact squash-merge result; merge only with required checks green on that exact head, another maker's APPROVED holding for it and the local merged-result test green, with head checks, gates and the hard limit unchanged.
34. Name a senior expert persona and a strict reviewer persona for every task, and log both with the skills used.
35. Builder and reviewer must come from different makers. The reviewer edits nothing and returns APPROVED or REJECTED with file, line, defect and exact fix for every finding. During the pilot of [decision 0110](docs/decisions/0110-approved-with-small-fixes-pilot.md) only, a reviewer may also return APPROVED-WITH-SMALL-FIXES on that record's items 2 to 4; every finding is still fixed before merge and closed by its script.
36. Run tests and record real exit codes. Never hide failure with `|| true`, `continue-on-error`, a missing pipefail setting, retries, skips or refreshed baselines.
37. Give every guard a planted violation. Write a failing test first where practical, then implement and prove the same test passes.
38. Record commands, full commit hash, counts, seeds, environment and evidence in [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md). Append corrections; never rewrite an earlier evidence row.
39. Record unresolved decisions, assumptions and random failures in [OPEN_ITEMS.md](OPEN_ITEMS.md). A green re-run alone does not close a random failure.
40. Push one at a time. Never push to the base branch and never force-push. Follow the task's review gate before merge.
41. Use the top model only for architecture, privacy/security rules, final disagreement rulings, hard debugging and phase reviews; use a strong mid-tier model for most planning, building and review, a fast cheap model for mechanical work, and other makers for independent review. Keep agent counts lean.
42. Before using a library newer than most training data (for example MapLibre GL JS 6, deck.gl 9.4, DuckDB 2.0, PostgreSQL 18, the current Claude API), read only the current official documentation for the parts the task uses, and confirm exact pins and written retreat pins at slice S1. Each brief names the documentation pages it used (decision 0054).
